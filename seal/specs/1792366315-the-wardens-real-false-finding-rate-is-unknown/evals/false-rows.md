The harness counts a report row as false when no `truth-` grader of its case
matches it (`evals/scripts/noninferiority.py#side_numbers`). Under
af9c924b's suite that is 43 of the baseline side's 55 rows and 31 of the
phase-3 side's 47. The sides are #957's committed
`seal/specs/1792126889-every-agent-is-written-anew-from-its-job/evals/baseline-warden.json`
and `warden-phase-3.json`. This file gives each of those 74 rows a class.

The classing is #971's first comment, made while the raw traces existed.
The traces are gone, so this file carries the comment's classes and does not
redo them. Each class whose reason rests on a fact about a tree is re-checked
here at the case's commits, and the command and what it gave are in the row.

## How to read a row

- **Side, Case, Run, Row** locate the row in the side JSON. Run is the
  0-based index of the run among the case's runs, and Row is the 0-based
  index of the line in that run's `specseal.report_rows`. The comment's
  `rN.FM` (run N, finding M of that run's printed record) is at the head of
  the Finding cell.
- **`location:`** is the side JSON's line, byte for byte.
- **Finding** is the comment's paraphrase of the finding. For every row it
  is the only record of the finding's wording: the committed rows keep the
  `location:` line alone, and the traces that held the rest are gone.
- **Class** is the comment's vocabulary:
  - `real`: a defect in the diff that the case does not list; a fix pass
    would act on it.
  - `real (truth-miss)`: a defect the case lists, written at a coordinate
    the truth's regex does not accept. The grader missed it.
  - `remark`: no defect behind it (taste, cost, or the convention followed).
  - `remark (artefact)`: true of the eval clone and not of the shipped
    diff. The seeded cases' `scaffold.sh` removes the work item's `rounds/`
    and fetches one commit, so citations of the shipped rounds look
    dangling.
  - `remark (fenced)`: a `location:` line inside a finding's fenced
    paste-ready block, which the harness's reader counts as a row.
  - `wrong`: the claim is false of the code.
  - `unclear`: the diff alone cannot settle it.
- **Reason** says why the class holds. A command marked executed was run on
  2026-10-19 in this repository and gave what the row says. A historical
  round record's yellow and white markers are written `should N` and
  `note N`. A part marked *read in #971's comment* rests on the comment
  alone.
