# Feature Specification: the routing question shows the four boxes only under `per axis`

<!-- seal/specs/<unix-epoch-seconds>-<slug>/spec.md — WHAT this work delivers and how we'll know.
The policy documents in docs/ outrank this file; cite them, don't restate. -->

Issue #911, milestone 59 (`release: 0.21.1`). The routing question is asked
as two questions in one `AskUserQuestion` call, and the harness shows every
question of a call at once. So a person who presses `automation` or `no work
item` — the two answers under which the four per-axis boxes mean nothing —
is shown the boxes anyway, at the start of nearly every run. This work asks
question 1 alone and puts the boxes in a second call only when the answer
was `per axis`. The guard's press reader already reads question 1 asked
alone as the press; it is pinned, not changed.

## Data & interfaces

**The one spelling.** Every file that states the shape carries this sentence,
verbatim after whitespace is collapsed:

> Ask question 1 alone, and the four boxes in a second call only under `per axis`.

The tests pin that string in `skills/implement/orchestration.md`,
`templates/claude-md-block.md` and `CLAUDE.md`, and refuse `two questions in
ONE `AskUserQuestion` call` in each of them and in `skills/implement/SKILL.md`.
`tests/test_chain_hooks_hardening.py` asserted the literal `in one call` in
`skills/implement/orchestration.md`. Under `per axis` the asking is two
calls, so that case's ground, the batch being one wait, does not hold; the
case now asserts the sentence *Question 1 goes in one call of its own*.
*Corrected 2026-10-13 after round 1 (🟡 2, ⬜ 8): this paragraph first kept
the bare literal on the ground that it still held.*

**Headings.** Two headings in `skills/implement/orchestration.md` move, and a
heading is a coordinate, so the move is named here:

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| `## Orchestrator: how the work is routed — two questions, one call, one file` | `## Orchestrator: how the work is routed — question 1 alone, the boxes only under per axis, one file` |
| `### Question 2 — \`multiSelect\`, meaningful only under *per axis*` | `### Question 2 — \`multiSelect\`, asked only under *per axis*` |

`### Question 1 — single-select` does not move. The act table rows in
§*Orchestrator: which of these acts runs itself* follow the headings, and the
one reference outside the file (`docs/worktree-guard-spec.md` §*Known limits*)
follows too.

**What the reader reads** (`hooks/worktree_consent.py#_routing_preset`,
unchanged): it walks every question of the result, skips any whose
`multiSelect` is not `False`, and matches the one whose options' leading
phrases are the three labels. A result with the boxes beside question 1 and
one with question 1 alone therefore reach the same branch. That is a reading
of the code; S5 is what makes it a fact.

**The second call** carries the same four boxes §*Question 2* already
specifies, with `multiSelect: true`, in the stated order. Nothing reads its
result: the declaration is what the session writes from it, as before.
