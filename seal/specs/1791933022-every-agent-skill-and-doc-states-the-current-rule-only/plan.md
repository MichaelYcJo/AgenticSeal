## Summary

The review chain is designed from the jobs. A round record is one file the
warden writes — the commit it reviewed, one section per finding with its
severity, location, verdict and paste-ready fix, its confirmations, what it
could not judge, and its probes — and the smith's fix pass answers each
finding by rewriting its `verdict:` line and writing its `answer:` line.
Nothing is generated, copied or derived: `round_record.py`, eleven fields,
four sections, the verdict table with its emoji marks and the reviewer's
separate report leave, and the check at the pull request reads five facts —
the declaration, the frame, the records, the broad gate's file, the sealer's
verdict — and of a record its lowercase labelled lines, each in one
spelling, refusing whatever it cannot read: an unanswered finding, a `fixed`
commit off the branch or touching nothing of its location, a finding closed
`fixed` or `rejected` that the next round neither confirms nor names as a
regression, a label spelt another way. The sealer becomes the final independent guarantor on
Opus: it runs the gate, re-runs a sample of the smith's claims, and reads the
framer's, the smith's, the warden's, the changelog's and the pull request's
stories against the issue and the tree, writing `guarantee.md`. That is this
item's whole scope, part A of #923. The five definitions and the contract
written anew from their jobs, the baseline and the proof that each rewrite is
not worse are part B, #957, framed on its own after 0.22.0 is installed: the
agents are written once, against the record this item ships, by agents that
already run it.
