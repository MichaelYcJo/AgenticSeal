**What breaks in 6 months with the chosen approach.** `Enforced by: nothing — <why>` is the escape hatch, so it is counted in `unenforced:<file>` and raising the count owes a declared reason; a session that writes `nothing` to pass still moves a number a reviewer sees. A target that resolves but is skipped on every leg, or asserts nothing, passes the resolution gate: the stated limit, with review as the reader. The paragraph reader opens on `**` and the list-item openers; a rule written as a plain sentence is not counted and is review's.
