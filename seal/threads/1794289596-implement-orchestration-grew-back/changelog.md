### Changed

- **`skills/implement/orchestration.md`, which every orchestrating session opens before its first edit, is back under 0.30.0's size (#1477).** It held 7,311 words at 0.37.1 and holds 5,233. The passages an orchestrator acts on only under a condition now stand in two files it opens on that condition, and the body names each file with the condition in one sentence. `skills/implement/routing-question.md` holds the routing question's two calls, what each answer writes, how a question of the batch names its answers, and what the batch does about the two language keys; the orchestrator opens it when it puts the batch of questions to a person, and where either language key is missing from `config.json`. `skills/implement/merge-order.md` holds the order a release's work items merge in and a late version move's verifying round; the orchestrator opens it when it starts a work item of a release that holds another, before the item's first edit. The body keeps the rows of the routing declaration, the branch a pull request lands on, and every heading another file cites except *Question 1*, which is now *Question 1, the mode* in `skills/implement/routing-question.md`.
- **The rule for a decision made after the frame is committed is six paragraphs of judgment, where it was one paragraph of ordered acts (#1477).** The paragraphs say what a decision is, who writes its fold, how far one fold reaches, what the orchestrator records and when, what a rejection leaves, and what is folded before the frame is committed. Every clause of the old paragraph stands in one of them. Two clauses are new. In a section the frame dates to the commit it read, a sentence that says what the work does or concludes is folded with the fact it states. The orchestrator's message to the framer names the fact a decision changes and says that its coordinates are the statements it has seen, not the whole list. The sentence on folding the version move into `frame/plan.md` *Operational impact* before the last phase is spawned stays in the body, beside the rule that gives the move to the orchestrator, because a release of one work item never opens `skills/implement/merge-order.md`.
- **A dated frame section's conclusions about the work are read against the present (#1477).** `skills/implement/SKILL.md` *Document layout — two roots, three lifetimes* says that a sentence of `frame/design.md` *Grounding* or `frame/plan.md` *Technical context* that says what the work does or concludes is no claim about the commit the framer read. A fold rewrites it with the fact it states, and a reader holds it against HEAD. `agents/proofer.md` and `agents/crafter.md` each exempted a dated section whole. Each now exempts its coordinates and statements of the tree, reads a sentence there on what the work does or concludes against the tree it reads, and cites that section.
- **The files that sent a reader to the moved passages name where they stand (#1477).** `README.md` and `README.ko.md` name `skills/implement/routing-question.md` for each answer of the routing question. `skills/implement/multi-repository.md` names it for the question's shape. `skills/commit-pr-convention/SKILL.md` and `skills/implement/bootstrap.md` cite it for the two language keys.

### Removed

- **Nine statements the body made of facts another place owns (#1477).** These are the paragraph **The first edit waits for the routing answer**, the paragraph **A wrong choice stays visible**, and the sentences on the mid-run question under `Automation` `yes`, on a question against a row, on `direct` writing no declaration, on CI reading the declaration for either review answer, on a wrong `Planning` answer, on where the framer runs, and on what the framer's hand-back names. The opening no longer lists what `skills/implement/SKILL.md` holds. The paragraph on where a migration comparison is recorded is one sentence citing `skills/legacy-parity/SKILL.md` *Recording the comparison* and `docs/running/the-review-and-parity-arms.md` *Parity arm*.
- **Two sentences of the ladder that said why a judgment was preferred to a count (#1477).** No party acts on them. The sentences after them, on naming the value a person waits on, stay.

`docs/running/the-review-and-parity-arms.md` *The declaration, and where the check went* cites `skills/implement/orchestration.md` *Question 1* until the settle after this release writes the citation of `skills/implement/routing-question.md` *Question 1, the mode*.
