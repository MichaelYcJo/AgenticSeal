### Fixed

- **The orchestrator's part of a pull request body now states a record's fact only as that record's own sentence or with the coordinate that holds it (#1290).** The orchestrator writes the first part of the body, above the copy of the work item's records. The file it reads at that step said that part restates no record and names a record's path where it needs a record's fact. No body merged into `release/v0.28.0` kept that rule, and the sealer withheld twice on a sentence of that part written from memory of the run. One was a count of a design's departures that the design does not give, one a request attributed to the owner with no record of it, and one a reading of a round's finding past what the round says. `skills/code-review/orchestration.md` *Orchestrator: the pull request opens once the sealer seals* now says four things. A sentence of that part that states a record's fact is the record's own sentence, or carries the record's path and the row, heading, finding or line that holds the fact. The orchestrator opens the record when it writes the sentence. A count, an "each", "every" or "all", and a decision attributed to a person carry the coordinate of what they count or of where the person's answer stands. A copied sentence carries its record's claim and no more, and the sealer still reads the record against the tree. `skills/writing-style/outside-the-review.md` points to that section in place of its own copy of the old rule, and `skills/commit-pr-convention/SKILL.md` *Pull request bodies* and `skills/implement/acts.md` now agree with it. No check is added, because whether a sentence agrees with its record is a question of meaning, which the sealer's reading answers.
