# Design: the orchestrator's part of a pull request body cites its records (#1290)

In 0.28.0 the sealer withheld twice on the orchestrator's own part of a pull request body. Both times the sentence was a summary of a record, written from memory of the run. The file the orchestrator reads at that step says "Your part restates no record: where it needs a record's fact, it names the record's path" (`skills/code-review/orchestration.md:369-370`). No body in 0.28.0 kept that rule (G4), and nothing says how a body that does state a record's fact keeps to the record. This work rewrites the rule in place. The orchestrator's part may state a record's fact, but only as the record's own sentence or with the record's coordinate, opened when the sentence is written. A count, an "each" or "all", and a decision given to a person carry the coordinate of what they rest on. No check is added.

## Scope

In:

1. `skills/code-review/orchestration.md` *Orchestrator: the pull request opens once the sealer seals*, the paragraph that opens **You write the pull request body after `trail/gate.md` is committed**. The sentence "Your part restates no record: where it needs a record's fact, it names the record's path." is rewritten in place to say four things:
   - A sentence of your part that states a record's fact is that record's own sentence, or carries the record's coordinate: its path, and the row, heading, finding or line that holds the fact.
   - You open that record when you write the sentence, after the last fix pass, and you do not write the fact from your memory of the run.
   - A count, an "each", "every" or "all", and a decision given to a person, such as "the owner asked", carries the coordinate of what it counts or of where the person's answer stands, such as `routing.md`, a `frame/questions.md` row's `Status` or an issue comment.
   - A copied sentence carries its record's claim and no more. The sealer reads the record against the tree, so the sentence adds no claim of its own.

   The list of what no record holds, before that sentence, stays as it is. Nothing else in the section changes. Leave *On the broad run's exit 1 spawn nothing* alone (G11).
2. `skills/writing-style/outside-the-review.md` *PR 본문에만 해당하는 것*, lines 27-29. The sentence "오케스트레이터는 그 줄 위에 기록에 없는 것만 쓰고, 기록에 있는 사실이 필요하면 다시 쓰지 않고 그 기록의 경로를 적는다." becomes a pointer, in Korean, to item 1's section as the place that says how the orchestrator's part states a record's fact. It restates none of item 1.
3. `skills/commit-pr-convention/SKILL.md` *Pull request bodies*, lines 240-242. "What the rounds found is copied from the round records, so the orchestrator's part does not restate it." becomes a sentence saying that what the rounds found is the copy's, copied from the round records, and that a round's fact in the orchestrator's part is stated as item 1's section says. The shape of the chain section and the pinned phrases of `tests/text/test_the_chain_section_has_one_shape.py` stay.
4. `skills/implement/acts.md`, the row *Orchestrator: the pull request opens once the sealer seals*, its last sentence "Nothing reads whether the orchestrator's part above that line restates a record; the sealer reads that part as one party among five". It becomes a sentence saying that nothing reads whether a sentence of the orchestrator's part carries the record it states, and that the sealer reads that part against the records and the tree, as one party among five. `Delivered by` stays `still a sentence`.
5. `evals/scripts/rule_cases.py:826-828`, the comment on `OWN_PART`. "the orchestrator's: what no record holds" is corrected to the rule as `## Rule changes` states it (contract rule 19). The fixture's text and the eval's behaviour do not change.
6. This work item's `changelog.md`: one `### Fixed` entry, written by the crafter, saying what the orchestrator's part of a pull request body now holds to and why. A `### Process changes` table follows, with one row in the shape of `templates/records/process.md` for the change in item 1, written by the orchestrator with the fragment (G12).

Out, each with its reason:

- **A check, a hook or an eval case.** G1. The issue rules a check out unless a script can read the question exactly, and agreement between a sentence and a record is meaning.
- **`agents/sealer.md`.** G13. The sealer already reads that part as claims.
- **`skills/code-review/scripts/pr_body.py`, and any change to what the copy holds or where it stands.** `plan.md` A2 weighs moving a record above the fold and rejects it.
- **The local memory note that held the practice.** It is outside the tree and is the owner's own file. Retiring it changes nothing this work builds.
- **`docs/the-review-record.md` edited in this pull request.** The policy root is not edited by a pull request. The change is stated in `## Rule changes`, and the settle writes it.
- **The doubled check line at `docs/the-review-record.md:272-273`.** It was seen while reading. It is not this work's, and the settle that writes G3's change passes the paragraph it closes.
