- **The installed plugin during this run.** The chain runs under 0.34.1, which ships no hook. This item's stamp is queued by the sealer's `seal stamp --guarantee` and drawn by the launcher the owner installed by hand on 2026-11-07 where it still stands, or by `seal stamp --from <path>` otherwise. 0.35.0 is installed before any session runs under the new shape.
- **What a person does after updating.** Nothing for the hooks: `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}` follows the install. A person who said yes to the stamp under 0.33.0 to 0.34.1 meets step 4b's *Needs action* at the first `/agentic:update` and removes the launcher and its `Stop` entry by hand; until they do, the launcher exits 0 at each turn's end and the stamp is drawn once whichever drawer claims it.
- **`dist` and the directory.** `hooks/` ships again, so the directory's `LOCAL_CODE_HOOK_COMMAND` refusal returns by design; the owner's decision of 2026-11-08 accepts that until a listing is possible, and the removal then undoes *Scope* item 4's list.
- **#1426.** Closes with this item as moot: the pull request body's closing line names it beside #1430, and the release's close-issues workflow closes both when the release reaches `main`.
- **Compatibility.** `seal stamp --install`, `--remove`, `--decline` and `--hook` disappear from `seal --help`; a session typing one gets argparse's refusal. A person typing `/agentic:start` gets the harness's unknown-skill answer; the README's *Your first work item* says to ask in plain words. A repository whose `hygiene.yml` pins an earlier release is unaffected.
