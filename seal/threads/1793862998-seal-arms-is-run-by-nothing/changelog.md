### Removed

- **`seal arms` is gone, with its cases, its document and the CI job that ran them, because no agent ran it and no record shows it run (#1393).** The command listed a module's arms and reported the ones no case kills. `skills/verify/scripts/arm_check.py`, `tests/skills/test_arm_check.py` and `skills/verify/seal-arms.md` are deleted, and `bin/seal arms` now exits 2 with argparse's `invalid choice: 'arms'`. `seal mutate` stays, and the crafter still runs it over each unit that adds behaviour. It had loaded `arm_check.py` by path for three functions: the bytecode clear, the byte-exact restore and the split of the `--tests` command line. Those three are now `skills/verify/scripts/mutation_check.py`'s own, with the same signatures and behaviour, and the four cases that held them moved into `tests/skills/test_mutation_check.py`. `.github/workflows/test.yml` loses the `arms-grammar` job, which ran only `tests/skills/test_arm_check.py` at Python 3.13 and 3.14. No CI leg now runs a Python newer than the 3.12 floor until #1065 puts the suite on 3.14, and no ruleset required the job. `docs/running/the-broad-gate.md` *A check that cannot fail is not a check* drops its first rule, that a module's arms are enumerated and the unkilled ones reported, with its check line. `docs/running/what-a-script-gates.md` and `docs/releasing/branch-and-release.md` stop naming the command and the job. `skills/verify/SKILL.md` now names `seal mutate` alone under the heading *A unit: `seal mutate`*, and `skills/verify/seal-mutate.md` no longer compares itself with `seal arms`. `CONTRIBUTING.md`'s list of CI jobs drops the job. Three cases of `tests/behaviour/test_a_document_that_names_a_script_says_how_to_reach_it.py` take `seal mutate` as their example of a script with a subcommand, and other tests and comments that named the command now say what stands. `.test_durations` loses the 100 entries of the cases that left.
