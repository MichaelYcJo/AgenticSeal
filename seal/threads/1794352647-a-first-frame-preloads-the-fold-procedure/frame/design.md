## Scope

In:

1. **A new file, `skills/implement/fold-and-reframe.md`.** It opens with a title in the shape of its siblings, `# implement, the framer's fold and reframe`, and a paragraph saying that the framer opens it before it answers a decision to fold into its committed frame, whether spawned or resumed, and before it answers a `regression:` the orchestrator routes to it, and that no other party acts from it.
