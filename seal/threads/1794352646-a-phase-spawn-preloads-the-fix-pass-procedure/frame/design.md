## Scope

In:

1. **A new file, `skills/implement/fix-pass.md`, holds the fix pass's procedure.** Its opening paragraph says who opens it and when: the crafter at step 9, and a session that fixes review feedback, from `skills/implement/SKILL.md` *5.*. It names `skills/code-review/orchestration.md` *Orchestrator: a phase is a fresh crafter, and a fix pass resumes the implementer* as the orchestrator's half. Its sections are the ones D1 lists, in that order. Each moved sentence keeps its words, with two exceptions. A sentence that speaks to "you" takes the fix pass as its subject (G14), as the form and the orchestrator's half already write it. A sentence that cites a passage of the file it leaves cites that passage by path. The close-block section cites the form's two paragraphs by their bold leads for the verdicts and the `location:` rule (G2) and `lib/fields.py` for their spelling. It states neither the values nor the rule. It keeps the moved rule's `check:` line.
2. **`agents/crafter.md` keeps one pointer.** Step 9 becomes the sentence "In a fix pass, open `skills/implement/fix-pass.md` and follow it in place of steps 5 to 8." The rule **A fix pass appends close blocks and writes no phase record.** leaves whole, its `check:` line with it. The *Job* paragraph, steps 1 and 10, and the rules *Your scratch is named for the work item and the phase.* and *Before you hand back, your range and the work item's records are read whole against HEAD.* stay as written.
3. **`skills/implement/SKILL.md` *5.* names the file.** The heading stays. The paragraphs at `:409-435` leave for the new file (D1). One paragraph in their place says that the fix pass, the crafter's or a session's, opens `skills/implement/fix-pass.md` before it judges the first finding. The paragraph at `:437-440` on draining the open rows is the orchestrator's and stays.
4. **`skills/code-review/orchestration.md` cites the form for what the block holds.** The bold sentence at `:51-55` drops "holding its verdict, its answer and, where its fix went to a path the finding does not name, a `location:` line naming it", and keeps "appends one close block to each open finding of `trail/rounds/round-N.md`", "it edits nothing else in the record" and its citation of the form.
5. **The cases read the text where it stands.**
   - `tests/text/test_a_moved_rule_leaves_its_definition.py`: the fix-pass case reads its clauses from `skills/implement/fix-pass.md`, in the wording item 1 gives them. Three clauses restate the form: "`fixed <commit>`, `rejected` or `deferred <home>`", "`deferred <where it was repaired>`" and "carries a `location:` line naming the one it did change". They leave the case's presence list. The case asserts that the verdict list and the words "carries a `location:` line naming" stand in none of `agents/crafter.md`, `skills/implement/fix-pass.md`, `skills/implement/SKILL.md` and `skills/code-review/orchestration.md`, that the form holds both, and that step 9 names the new file. The case's name and docstring say what it reads after the move.
   - `tests/text/test_the_rules_have_one_owner.py`: row 15's carrier `IMPLEMENT` becomes the new file, and the comment at `:277` cites the new file's heading.
   - `tests/behaviour/test_docs_line_wrap.py#COVERED` gains the new file, with a comment naming #1471.
6. **The fragment and the version.** `changelog.md` with its `### Process changes` table (G12), and the version move *Operational impact* in `frame/plan.md` names.

Out:

- **`docs/forms/the-round-record.md`.** It already states both facts (G2), so no `docs/` text changes and no rule changes.
- **The other statements that the fix pass writes its closes last and commits none.** The form states it at `:96-101`, and `skills/code-review/orchestration.md:80-84` states it for the orchestrator, which commits on it; `tests/text/test_the_rules_have_one_owner.py:280-282` pins the latter. The new file keeps the step the fix pass takes. `frame/plan.md` *Alternatives considered* row 8 holds the grounds.

Its count of two lines predates #1198's close block, which holds a third line where the fix went elsewhere. The orchestrator files it, since it is a `docs/` sentence this work does not edit.
- **What the rest of the release changes in `agents/crafter.md`**: the rule *Before you hand back …* and contract rules 12 and 19 (#1472), the restated contract rules and the scratch rule (#1474 with #1503), and the `check:` lines (#1475).
