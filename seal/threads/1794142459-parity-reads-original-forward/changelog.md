### Fixed

- **In a migration, the parity method reads the original forward at the frame and at review, so an item the original sends and the port leaves out is a finding (#15).** `skills/legacy-parity/SKILL.md` *The original's coordinates* started every comparison at the coordinates the design cited, so an item nobody cited was compared by nobody. A port shipped two of its original's thirteen response items missing that way, and a person found them by hand. Now, for each entry point the work ports, the framer lists the original's output surface in `frame/design.md` *Grounding*: each response field, query axis and error code the original sends, each with its coordinate at the baseline. Where the original does not resolve, the *Grounding* says `original not found` with what was tried. `agents/framer.md` step 4 sends the framer to that section. The tracer starts at the original's entry point and opens the design's coordinates there (`agents/tracer.md` step 3), and its report names each item of the surface the design's list lacks, with its coordinate (step 6). At review, the proofer's stage 1 walks the original's surface from its entry point forward to the port, and opens the design's list at each item (`skills/legacy-parity/SKILL.md` *Parity review*). An item the original sends and the port does not is `differs` by default, and `grounded` only where a policy clause is quoted for leaving it out. An item that the design's list and the port both lack is one finding with that same verdict, located in `frame/design.md`, and in the port's entry point as well where the verdict is `differs`. Each item walked stands in the round record, as a finding or as a `## Confirmations` line naming the item and both coordinates, so the next round opens those lines instead of listing the surface again. Under `straight to the PR` the sealer walks the surface the same way and records it in the `original:` line, as before. No record format, field or command changes.
- **The response-shape axis of a review names the direction it is walked in (#15).** In `skills/code-review/SKILL.md` *Comparison axes*, the row's fields are walked from the reference's fields to the code's. A paragraph under the table states the default: the proofer looks in the code for each field the reference says the response carries, and a field it does not find there is a finding by default, as is a field the reference leaves out and the code sends. The reference is what stage 1 reads above the code: the policy documents and the work item's `frame/design.md`, and in a migration the original as well. This holds in every repository, with or without `seal/parity.md`.

Updating: nothing to do. A design framed before this release is not framed again. Its next review walks from the original and locates in `frame/design.md` what the design's list lacks, and in the port as well where the port lacks it too with no grounds for leaving it out.
