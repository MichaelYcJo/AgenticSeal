## Not done

`--from` at a directory above the `seal/` root, such as `--from .` at the
repository's top, is accepted, and the listing then holds the root's own
files. Leaving them out would be a second rule beside design *Scope* item 2,
which refuses a path at or under the root and nothing above it. The settling
session reads every listed document whole and proposes a record of the root
`skip`, so nothing of the root reaches the policy root unread. Whether a
listing should leave the root's files out is a frame question for the
proofer or the orchestrator, not a build one.
