## Not done

`--from` at a directory above the `seal/` root, such as `--from .` at the
repository's top, is accepted, and the listing then holds the root's own
files. Leaving them out would be a second rule beside design *Scope* item 2,
which refuses a path at or under the root and nothing above it. The settling
session reads every listed document whole and proposes a record of the root
`skip`, so nothing of the root reaches the policy root unread. Whether a
listing should leave the root's files out is a frame question for the
proofer or the orchestrator, not a build one.

`settle.py`'s `DOCUMENTS_HEADING` cites `skills/settle/SKILL.md` and not
its section *Directories a person names*, which phase 2 wrote. Naming the
section would change a line the dry run prints and a heading
`tests/skills/test_settle.py` reads, in a file phase 2's row does not
deliver. The heading already says that the settling session proposes and
the person marks, and the skill's procedure links the section.

The tagline of `README.md` and `README.ko.md`, "without asking anyone" and
"아무에게도 묻지 않고", speaks of the whole workflow and not of the settle,
and it stands as it was. Design A9 reads the sentences that say the settle
asks nobody, and the tagline is not one of them.
