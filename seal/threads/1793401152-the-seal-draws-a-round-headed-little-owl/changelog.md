### Changed

- **The stamp presses a round-headed little owl into a red wax seal (#1174).** The owl the stamp carried from 0.23.1 had raised head corners that read as ear tufts, so it looked like a horned owl. The owner chose a little owl with a round head, ringed eyes, a spotted breast and an olive branch, drawn for the terminal's 28-cell grid. The disc around it is now a red wax seal: an edge that wobbles, a lip lit from the upper left with a gloss streak, a step ring lit the other way, and the pressed field. It replaces the rim and the groove, and every cell is one of fifteen colours. The letter twin, which a console that cannot draw half-blocks prints, writes the wax in lower-case letters and the owl in the sprite's own letters. The stamp is smaller than before: 5,725 UTF-16 units for the widest reference panel with its label, against 5,959. A stamp queued by 0.25.0 is drawn with the new disc. The plugin's icon and the release note's seal image are drawn from the new vector seal, `assets/seals/owl.svg`. `assets/seals/` keeps the horned owl's files as `horned-owl.*` beside the new entry. The AgenticSeal name, the owl emblem and its seal drawings are not licensed under the MIT License. A new `TRADEMARKS.md` says so, both READMEs link to it, and the code stays under the MIT License in `LICENSE`. A user's installed plugin holds `TRADEMARKS.md` as it holds `LICENSE`, and the plugin loader reads neither.
