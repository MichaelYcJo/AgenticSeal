### Changed

- **The three lines a round answers a closed finding on are defined in one place (#1476).** `docs/running/the-run.md`, `skills/code-review/orchestration.md`, `skills/code-review/SKILL.md` and `agents/proofer.md` each said what `confirms:`, `reopens:` and `regression:` mean, and two of them stated the two routes of a regression. `skills/code-review/SKILL.md` *Earlier rounds*, which the proofer opens at step 1, now defines the three lines, the finding a `regression:` line names and what its grounds hold. The other three files cite that section. No line's meaning changes.
- **Each part of the verifying-round rule has one owner (#1476).** `agents/proofer.md` keeps the bound of a verifying round, and now holds the sentence on a new unit in the fix diff, which `skills/code-review/orchestration.md` held. `skills/code-review/orchestration.md` *Orchestrator: the run ends with a verifying round* keeps the orchestrator's judgment: when a round counts, the weighing at the third round, the signals, the stop row and the routes of a regression. `docs/running/the-run.md` *The run* keeps the policy and cites the orchestrator's half in one sentence.
- **The core's row *No fix that no round reads* states both conditions on which the sealer, and no round, reads a change (#1476).** The cell said the sealer reads a change "where it touched the work item's records alone". It now adds "and each finding it closed is located in those records alone", which the pull request check already held: a finding located in code is read by a round even where its fix touched records alone. The check's behaviour does not change. The statements of that condition in `docs/running/the-run.md`, `skills/code-review/orchestration.md`, `docs/checks/the-pull-request-check.md` and `skills/implement/orchestration.md` now name the row and state no condition of their own.
- **The proofer's definition says who reads a quoted notice (#1476).** Step 7 of `agents/proofer.md` asked a finding taken from a notice to quote the notice "so the settle can count what the notices caught", and no settle document reads a notice. The instruction to quote stays. Its reason now names the reader the quotes have: they are the record `skills/core/SKILL.md` *The rule a mechanism is kept against* weighs the notices by, counted with `git grep -F` on a notice's words.
- **`tests/text/test_the_rules_have_one_owner.py` holds the new owners (#1476).** `RULES` gains a row for the three lines and a row for the core's cell, each with a link from every file that cites the owner. `test_a_close_that_does_not_hold_reopens_it` no longer requires four files to name `confirms:` and `regression:`. It requires that of `skills/code-review/SKILL.md`, and requires of each of the four files that a sentence naming those two names `reopens:` too.

### Removed

- **One of two identical `check: last-record-at-head` lines in `docs/checks/the-pull-request-check.md` (#1476).** The line that stays names the same marker.
