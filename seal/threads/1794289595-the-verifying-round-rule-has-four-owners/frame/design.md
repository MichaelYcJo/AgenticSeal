## Scope

In scope:

1. **`skills/code-review/SKILL.md` *Earlier rounds* defines the three lines, and no other file of the preload or of `code-review` does.** The section says that every finding the round before closed `fixed <commit>` or `rejected` is named this round by exactly one line. `confirms:` names it where the close holds. `regression:` stands on a new finding where the defect is in a unit a fix pass's own commits added or changed and those commits introduced it. `reopens:` stands on a new finding in every other case where the close does not hold: a rejection that was wrong, or a `fixed` commit that left the defect standing, in the unit that commit changed or in another. A `regression:` line names the finding whose fix made the unit, as `round-<N>#<n>`, or `round-<N>` for a fix pass no finding named. Its grounds open with the unit, then hold one sentence that sets the fix beside the frame, from the phase's `frame/plan.md` row and `frame/design.md`: what the frame says, what the fix did, and where the defect came from. No label carries that sentence. A defect in a unit a build phase made is new work and takes neither line, even where the round that finds it reads that phase. The sentence "Where you weigh an earlier finding, re-run its `evidence: executed` line rather than re-deriving the claim" stays word for word. *The record* keeps where each line stands in a record and the phrase "where `reopens:` stands", and defines no line.
