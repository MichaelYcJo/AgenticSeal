## Data & interfaces

### The release check's words

The crafter writes the final wording under these constraints, and S1 and S7 pin it.

- **The error.** It opens as it does now: the version is a patch, and the branch carries N `feat:` subject(s), each named. Then, in this order: a `feat:` makes a minor; a `feat:` filed or moved into a patch-numbered milestone moves to the planned minor whose goal it shares, before its squash; one squashed onto this branch stays in its range, since this reading counts it even after a revert; so here the repair is to rename the milestone and every later planned `release:` milestone one step forward, cut this branch again under the new name, and move `plugin.json` to the new version in the new branch's first pull request; the citation; and "The prefix states what the change is and is never lowered to fit the number". The message names no force-push and no ruleset, which are this repository's settings and not facts the script reads.
- **The minor line.** `{version} carries no feat: and ships as the minor it was named, since its branch is cut under that name`. The bound it states is *Rule changes*' "Once a branch is cut, its name is never pulled back".
- **The docstrings.** The module docstring's second-question paragraph names the move and the rename as the policy states them, and its sentence "the repair is the same push" becomes "a `feat:` squashed here stays counted after a revert, so the repair the error names for it is the rename". The test module's docstring states no repair and stays as it is.
