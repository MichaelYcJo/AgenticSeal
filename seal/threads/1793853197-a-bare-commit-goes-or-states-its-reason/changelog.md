### Changed

- **No test, workflow or script comment names a commit by its bare hash any more, and a bare hash outside `seal/` that names a commit no `main` or release branch holds is refused (#1359).** Eighty-six comment and docstring lines in 29 files named a commit by its hash alone, most to say at which commit a test was seen failing or who measured a figure where. Thirty-five of those hashes named a commit that no clone holds once its feature branch is squashed, so a reader sent to one found nothing. Each line was read for what its reader acts on today. Seventy-three said where a case was seen failing or who found something, and that clause is gone, with the rule left standing in the sentence. Four held the grounds of a behaviour, which now stand in the sentence. Nine leaned on a past state and now say in the present what the case or the constant holds; two of them date a phrase's old wording by #639. `.github/workflows/test.yml` and `skills/implement/scripts/seal.py` change comments only. Thirty lines keep a hash as data: the seeded proofer cases' names and the commits their scaffolds fetch, a key in `.test_durations`, and test fixtures, each a commit `main` holds. `tests/text/test_a_cited_commit_outlives_the_squash.py` now also reads each bare hex token of seven to forty digits outside `seal/`, keeps the ones a single `git cat-file --batch-check` resolves as commits, and refuses one that no `main` or release ref contains, as it already refused a `<commit>:<path>` citation. A token that names no commit, such as a synthetic fixture value, is not read, and the records under `seal/` keep the commits they name. The refusal gives the file, the line and the hash, and says to state in the sentence what its reader needs or to cite the issue that holds it. The module's tree case takes about one second longer on a macOS machine.
