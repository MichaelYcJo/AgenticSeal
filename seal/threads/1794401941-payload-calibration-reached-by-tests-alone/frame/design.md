## Data & interfaces

The command after the cut:

```
payload_meter.py [--root DIR] [--agent NAME]... [--json]
```

Exit codes: 0 measured; 1 no `agents/*.md` under the root; 2 a module the meter loads is absent beside it, or the arguments were unusable.

The functions that change their signature: `measure(root, home, agents=(), config=None)`, `composition(root, home, name, definition, user=None)`, `pair_rows(root, home, user)`, `session_composition(root, home, user)`, `file_row(root, rel, display, share)`. Their only callers outside the file are `release_report.py#preloads`, `tests/github/test_release_report.py`'s stand-in, `tests/skills/test_payload_meter.py` and `tests/behaviour/test_a_section_marked_for_one_role_reaches_only_that_role.py`, which calls `agents_in` and `frontmatter` and neither changes.

The JSON loses the keys `ratios`, `baseline`, `calibrated_from`, `calibration` and `delta` at the top, `prefix` in an agent, and `sections` in a row. No reader outside the tests reads any of them (G3, G4).
