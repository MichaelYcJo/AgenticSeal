**Decisions made while building, each with its grounds.**

- `chain_check.round_read` is the one place a round record's blocks are placed. It reads the record once through `hooks/lib/fields.py`, splits `## Findings` at its `### ` headings, and returns `Parsed`: the findings, the errors, the round block, the held numbers, the `confirms:` references, the section and spans, and the blocks outside and inside each finding. `round_of` and `record_findings` keep their names and return shapes (*Scope* item 6), and `pr_body.py`, `original_of` and `noninferiority.py` read `Parsed` instead of placing blocks again. Judged against `skills/core/SKILL.md` *The rule a mechanism is kept against*: a fact has one source.
- A record with no round block, where the reader refused nothing and no block stands out of its place, is refused with `NO_ROUND_BLOCK` alone and read no further. This is the one message *User scenarios* asks for a record from before the change. A record whose round block the reader refused at a line is still read, so its findings' refusals come in the same run.
- A refusal of a block's line inside a finding is led by the finding's heading, and the finding is not returned. A comment line, a fence that never closes and a fence the walk cannot place are refusals of a line outside every block, so they leave the finding read. Below a fence that never closes, or one the walk cannot place, no block is read, and a finding reaching that line is read for the blocks above it and refused for nothing it lacks. Grounds: `docs/the-review-record.md` *What the check reads at the pull request* says each refusal names the finding's heading, and the old reader read a finding whose `verdict:` stood above an unclosed fence.
