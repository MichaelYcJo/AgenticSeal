The failure scenario of the chosen approach, in six months. A proofer or a fix pass writes a field outside a block, so the check refuses the finding for lacking its block, and the round costs one more preflight. The refusal says where the field goes, and the proofer's own `chain_check.py --worktree` run catches it before the hand-back. A worse failure: a proofer writes a whole finding inside another fence, and the finding is not read at all. That is the limit #1121 R3 left, and the orchestrator's read before the commit is still what catches it. What would show either happening is the settle's reading of *Judgments of the run* and the round records' findings located only in records.
