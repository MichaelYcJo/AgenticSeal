### Changed

- **No test, workflow or eval script cites a file at a commit any more (#1355).** Twenty-five comment, docstring and fixture lines in sixteen test files and in `.github/workflows/test.yml` held a citation written as `<commit>:<path>`. Most named a work item's record under `seal/specs/`, a directory the tree no longer has, so each cost its reader about a hundred characters and opened only through `git show`, and nothing could tell when the cited text stopped meaning what the sentence said. Each line was read for what its reader acts on today. Ten said where a rule or a case came from, and that clause is gone, with the rule left standing in the sentence. Six held the grounds of a value or a decision: the grounds now stand in the sentence, and two name the issue that holds them, #422 and #667. Seven leaned on a past file's text, such as a spec's scenario numbering or its table of shapes, and now say what they need in the present, citing the issue where its page holds it: #664, #667 or #871. Two were inputs to a test, and each test file now builds that line from the commit and the path apart, so the value the test reads is the same string. The workflow's diff changes comments only. No check is added to keep the form out. `tests/text/test_a_cited_commit_outlives_the_squash.py` still refuses a citation of a commit that no `main` or release branch holds, and that is the one such citation that sends a reader nowhere. Records under `seal/` keep their citations as written.
