### Changed

- **The settle closes an open row whose measurement nothing on record depends on, with the reason, and opens no issue for it (#1045).** At a settle, every open `## Not verified` row of a released work item is decided before its directory goes. A row that named a measurement nobody had taken was re-homed as an issue, whatever waited on its answer. The 0.22.4 settle opened 27 such issues, #1061 to #1087, and 22 of them were closed as not planned. Step 6 of `skills/settle/SKILL.md` *The settling session's procedure* now has a fourth kind, *a measurement no record asks for*, placed before *a measurement nobody has taken*. A row takes the first kind that fits, so it is closed with `closed` and a reason naming what the answer would have decided, and nothing is opened. *A measurement nobody has taken* now covers only a measurement that a failure, a report or a decision on record depends on. On record means a record other than the row itself: an open issue, a failed run, a review finding, or a rule or design that rests on the answer. Such a row is still re-homed as an issue, or as a `seal/follow-up.md` row in a repository with no tracker, and the issue's body now also names the record that needs each row's answer. The settling session decides each row's kind. The dry run's heading above the open rows names the four kinds in the table's order and gives no row a kind. The rule is in force from this merge through the work item's rule changes. The settle after the release writes it into `docs/the-record-layout.md` *The fold, and what tells it from a deletion*.
