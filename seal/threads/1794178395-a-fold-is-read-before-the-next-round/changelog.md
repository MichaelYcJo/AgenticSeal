### Changed

- **A decision made after the frame is committed is folded into the frame by the framer, which reads it before the next round (#1442).** The orchestrator used to rewrite the frame sentence a decision changed, and then read its own fold commit against HEAD. Four regressions in 0.27.3, 0.29.0 and 0.35.0 traced to such a fold: a fix pass built on the fold's words, and the next round found the defect the fold had written. Now `skills/implement/orchestration.md` *Orchestrator: the order inside a ticket* has the orchestrator send every pending decision to the framer in one message, each with its grounds and coordinates, before the spawn that will read it. A decision is anything that changes what the frame says the work is or why, such as a round finding whose route changes the frame, an owner's remark, an issue comment, a prevention home, a scope a spawn gives beyond a plan row, or the version move. The orchestrator resumes the work item's framer while its session still exists, and otherwise spawns a fresh framer with the decisions and the commit at which the frame was last written. It writes the decision's row of `frame/plan.md` *Judgments of the run* first and leaves it uncommitted, so the framer reads the row with `plan.md`. Then it commits the framer's changes and the row together. `agents/framer.md` *Rules* now names the framer's two routes back, a fold and a regression. On a fold, the framer answers each decision with agree, agree with a change, or reject with grounds, and writes a rejection as a row of *Alternatives considered*. It folds an owner's decision as the owner made it, opens each coordinate a decision cites, and rewrites in place every sentence the decision changes, reading each frame file whole. It writes no line under its mark, no Status cell and no judgment row. A statement that a crafter's hand-back names as contradicted by HEAD stays the orchestrator's to fold, and no framer is spawned for it. `skills/implement/SKILL.md` *3. The SDD file set*, the prevention-home paragraph and the version-move sentence of *Orchestrator: how the work is routed* now say that the framer folds. No check, hook or mark is added.

Updating: nothing to do.
