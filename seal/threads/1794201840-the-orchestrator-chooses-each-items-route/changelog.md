### Added

- **The routing question has a fourth answer, `agentic`, under which the orchestrator chooses each work item's route (#1459).** Question 1 put the route to the person for every item, and the ladder marked the full chain for nearly every change, so a two-line fix paid for a framer, a crafter, review rounds, a broad run and a sealer. Under `agentic` the orchestrator reads each item's issue and the tree and chooses which of the framer, `crafter` and `proofer` run and whether the run may stop to ask. A change it judges small enough, such as a two-line fix that touches no gate, hook or check script, takes `direct`: the session writes it with no work item, and the commit's body holds one sentence of grounds above the `Seal-Routing: no work item` trailer. `agentic` comes first in question 1 and carries the ` (Recommended)` mark every time; the ladder's rung is one signal the orchestrator weighs and no longer picks the mark. A new subsection, `skills/implement/orchestration.md` *Under agentic*, holds what the orchestrator weighs for `direct` and for each row, the three combinations of rows that do not stand, where the choice is written, and that a route is raised when the work outgrows it and never lowered. For a work item, the orchestrator writes the rows into `routing.md`, whose line reads `Answered <date> by the orchestrator, before the first edit.`, with each row's grounds under *Why this way*. No parser, check, trailer value or row changes: a declaration the orchestrator chose parses as one a person chose.
- **The three existing answers are renamed as modes: `automation` is `complete`, `per axis` is `manual`, and `no work item` is `direct`.** Each keeps its behaviour, and each description now says what its answer does. `direct`'s says that the session writes the change itself, with no agent, no work item, no frame, no review, no seal and no `routing.md`. The trailer keeps its value, `Seal-Routing: no work item`, because it records that a commit belongs to no work item, which a settle's commit and a durations refresh carry too. The `Automation` row keeps its name and its answers. `README.md` and `README.ko.md` list the four answers. Sentences that said "the direct answer" or "the direct route" for `Review` `straight to the PR` now name `straight to the PR`. The policy root's sentences on the routing answers, two in `docs/running/the-review-and-parity-arms.md`, one in `docs/running/the-run.md` and one in `docs/releasing/release-checklist.md`, change at the settle through this work item's rule changes.
- **The sealer and the settle read a route the orchestrator chose.** `agents/sealer.md` step 4 reads `routing.md` *Why this way* against the diff where the orchestrator chose the rows, and withholds where the diff reaches what the grounds do not describe. `skills/settle/SKILL.md` step 5 reads the release's chosen routes and `no work item` commits against the later commits that name them, and rewrites *Under agentic* where one kind of route keeps letting defects through.

Updating: nothing to do. A declaration already committed parses as before, and a person who answered `automation`, `per axis` or `no work item` finds the same routes under `complete`, `manual` and `direct`.
