## Data & interfaces

`chain_check.py#located_in_records(item, f)` keeps its name and signature. Its result changes from "every path of `f.paths` lies under `item/`, and there is one" to "one or more paths of `f.located` lie under `item/`". `Finding.located` is the field `#finding_of` already fills, the finding block's `location:` paths in the order written.

The check's output keeps its lines. It prints `chain_check.py#VERIFYING_ROUND`'s refusal for fewer findings of a last record, and for no finding it passes today.

No field, line keyword, subcommand, hook or exit code is added. The two commands of *Scope* item 6 are git's own.
