## Scope

The work makes one row splitter and one line split, and moves every reader in `lib/`, and the two readers outside it that the issue names, onto them.

1. **`lib/tables.py#table_cells` reads a row whose closing pipe is left off, as cmark-gfm does (G8, G11).** A row is a line that begins with `|` after at most three spaces. Its cells are the texts between unescaped pipes, and the text after the last unescaped pipe is one more cell unless `str.strip` empties it, a lone backslash that ends the line among such text (`CELL`'s third alternative). An escaped pipe stays one literal pipe (`unescaped`). A line that holds a pipe after an even run of backslashes stays None (`EVEN_ESCAPED_PIPE`), and so does a line that holds nothing after its leading pipe. The `CELL` comment's paragraph on the one narrowed shape (`:50-55`) is rewritten to what the reader now does with that shape, and `gfm_table`'s docstring stops calling a row without its closing pipe a refusal.
2. **`lib/routing.py#table_rows` splits each line through `table_cells`**, and skips a line `tables.DELIMITER_ROW` matches. It keeps its own walk, `shown`, and its rule that a row of two cells or more is read as its first two. `lib/routing.py` imports `tables` beside `blocks`, and a copy without `lib/tables.py` raises an `ImportError` whose sentence names that file, as the copy without `lib/blocks.py` does at `:42-55`.
3. **`lib/lines.py#split_row` goes, and its callers read `table_cells`.** `lib/gaps.py#parse_section` imports it from `tables`. `skills/settle/scripts/fold_check.py#index_rows` loads `lib/tables.py` through `lib/sibling.py`, and its docstring at `:37-39` names the reader it now uses. `skills/code-review/scripts/chain_check.py#table_rows` goes, and the case at `tests/skills/test_chain_check.py:4934` asks `table_cells` over the same lines. `lib/lines.py#is_separator` stays: `lib/gaps.py:152` asks it of cells `visible` has already cleaned, which no line pattern can be asked. `split_row`'s cases in `tests/lib/test_lines.py` move to a `Test_table_cells` class in `tests/lib/test_tables.py`.
4. **Every reader hands the walk the lines `blocks.gfm_lines` gives.** `lib/tables.py#gfm_table` splits its text with `blocks.gfm_lines`. `fence_map`, `hidden_lines` and `unfenced` in `lib/tables.py`, and `lib/routing.py#shown`, take LINES alone. `lib/routing.py#table_rows` and `skills/verify/scripts/pact_check.py#near_headers` and `#first_table` split with `gfm_lines`.
5. **The bridge goes.** `lib/blocks.py#walk_text` and `#_starts` go. `lib/tables.py#gfm_line_starts`, `GFM_LINE_ENDS` and `gfm_table`'s two refusals that name a character only Python ends a line at go, because a GFM line never holds a second reader line. The markers search 1 lists go with the `splitlines` calls under them. Each docstring and comment that describes two splits says there is one: `lib/blocks.py:66-71` and `:400-418`, `lib/tables.py:118-127` and `:439-446`, `lib/routing.py:105-111`, and `lib/lines.py:47-53`, with `lib/blocks.py`'s module docstring on inline HTML and its list of uncertain contexts, `lib/tables.py#hidden_lines`' list of uncertain contexts, and the `ALPHABET` and `FOUND` comments in `tests/lib/test_blocks.py`.
6. **The cases follow the one split.** `tests/commonmark_oracle.py#hidden_text` answers for each CommonMark line, and `as_read` in `tests/lib/test_blocks.py` splits with `gfm_lines`. A case whose guard the work removes goes with its guard. A case whose shape still reads is re-pinned to the new reading, and each new reading equals cmark-gfm's or is a refusal. `tests/lib/test_tables.py#test_a_backslash_against_a_pipe_is_the_one_shape_the_escape_narrows` is re-pinned as `test_a_backslash_against_a_pipe_is_an_escaped_pipe`, since item 1 reads that pipe as escaped. This holds for the U+2028 cases of `tests/lib/test_tables.py` at `:778-883` and `:1081-1100`, `tests/lib/test_blocks.py`'s `Test_walk_text` at `:352-420`, `tests/lib/test_routing.py:188-200` and `tests/behaviour/test_the_readers_hide_what_a_renderer_hides.py`. `tests/test_commonmark_oracle.py`'s break case becomes `test_the_oracle_ends_a_line_where_commonmark_does`, and `tests/skills/test_pact_check.py` gains two rows on the line a near or second header is counted at after a U+2028.
7. **`skills/settle/scripts/passages.py#words` reads fences through `lib/lines.py#readable`**, loaded through the `READER` it already loads, and drops heading lines from those lines. `FENCE` and `_lines` go, and the module docstring names the walk it now reads. A case in `tests/skills/test_passages.py` pins a four-space-indented fence run at the top level of a document: the words after it are read.

## Data & interfaces

- `lib/tables.py#table_cells(line)`: a tuple of stripped cells, or None. Scope item 1 states what changes, a trailing lone backslash among it. `gfm_table`, `opens_table`, `pact_check.py#near_headers` and `#first_table`, and `rule_changes.py:397` read it, and each then reads a row without its closing pipe where it refused or passed over one before. `rule_changes.py:397` then accepts such a row as a `to` that `settle --draft` places, and cmark-gfm renders it as a row (G8).
- `lib/tables.py#fence_map(lines)`, `#hidden_lines(lines)` and `#unfenced(lines)`: the `text` parameter goes, and LINES are `gfm_lines` of the file. The answers are unchanged where the file holds none of the eight characters G9 names, which is every tracked `.md` file.
- `lib/tables.py#gfm_table(text, header, beside=False)`: same signature. Its two refusals about a character only Python ends a line at go. A text that held one now meets another refusal or a read row, as S4 states, and the cases that pin the old sentences are re-pinned (contract rule 14).
- `lib/routing.py#shown(lines)` and `#table_rows(text)`: `shown` loses `text`. `table_rows` returns the same list of label and value pairs on every committed `routing.md` (S7), and differs on the shapes S1 to S3 name and on three no committed declaration holds: a row indented four spaces or a tab is not read, a row holding a pipe after an even run of backslashes is not read, and a row whose first cell is empty is read with the label `''`, where the old separator test skipped it.
- `lib/blocks.py#walk_text` goes, and a reader calls `walk(gfm_lines(text))`, as `lib/fields.py#read` does at `:337-338`.
- `lib/lines.py#split_row` goes. `is_separator` stays.
- `skills/code-review/scripts/chain_check.py#table_rows(reader, lines)` goes.
- `skills/settle/scripts/passages.py#words(text)`: same signature, its fences read by `lib/lines.py#readable`. `FENCE` goes, and no other module reads it (executed: `git grep -nE 'passages.*FENCE|\.FENCE\b' -- '*.py'` prints nothing).
