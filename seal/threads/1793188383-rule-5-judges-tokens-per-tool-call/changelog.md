### Changed

- **Rule 5 of the behaviour evaluation fails a rewrite only where its total tokens and its tokens per tool call both rose by more than 7% of the baseline's (#992).** Before, `evals/scripts/noninferiority.py#verdict` read tokens per tool call alone and failed any rise. A side that made fewer calls and read fewer tokens in all could fail it, and at two runs a case a rise of under one percent decided it. Where either figure stays within the margin, rule 5 now passes, so the call count moves the verdict in neither direction. The margin is the new constant `TOKEN_MARGIN = 0.07`, sized from the one recorded spread between two takes of one text, 6.8 points of tokens per tool call. A figure exactly at the margin passes. The line rule 5 writes when it fails now reads `the new side read <tokens> tokens across every model over <calls> tool calls, <figure> a call, against the baseline's <tokens> over <calls>, <figure> a call: the total and the figure per call both rose by more than 7% (rule 5)`. Total tokens now judge through rule 5, and tokens per true find, spend and wall seconds still judge nothing. `evals/README.md` *The behaviour suite* states the new rule. A record's rows and labels are unchanged, so every committed record still parses.
- **`noninferiority.py check` on an older comparison record can now disagree with the verdict the record states (#992).** The 17 committed comparisons of #943, #957, #967, #968, #969, #975, #976 and #985 were read again under the new rule, with no paid run. Rule 5 now fails one of them, #957's framer side, whose total tokens rose 49.7% and whose tokens per tool call rose 18.9%. Three records that failed on rule 5 alone now compute `pass`: #967's framer side, #967's warden side and #943's second sealer side. `check` on each of the three prints "it says `fail` and its numbers give `pass`" and exits 1. No committed record was edited.
