**G9's method.** In a `git clone --no-local` under the scratchpad, a work item is counted for the first tag of `v0.27.0` to `v0.30.1` whose `seal/threads/` holds it and whose previous tag does not. Its `routing.md` and round records are read at that tag through the readers `.github/scripts/finding_counts.py` loads (`hooks/lib/routing.py`, `skills/code-review/scripts/chain_check.py#record_findings`), and no record was refused. A finding counts where its finding block carries a `regression:` line. A finding is in *records* where every path of its finding block's `location:` lies under the work item's directory, as `finding_counts.py` reads it. It is in *the fix's diff* where every such path lies under the directory or among the files `git diff --name-only <a>..<b>` lists for the `fixes <a>..<b>` range of the round record that holds it. Those commits were read from each pull request's `refs/pull/<n>/head`, fetched from the host, since the squash dropped them. The probe is gone, as contract rule 7 asks. Run the same way over a later release's records, it gives the reading this one is the baseline for.

## Scope

In:

1. **Contract rule 12, `skills/agent-contract/SKILL.md:151-165`, rewritten in place.** The first four sentences stay. The sentences from "Where the class ranges over an unbounded domain" to the end are rewritten so that they say five things. First, the reach of a party's own fix is a closed set: its own diff, and each file of the work item's directory that states the present, read whole, which is the second method; a phase record and a round record state the commit they name, and HEAD contradicts neither. Second, a list of a closed set is never a sample. Third, a sample stays a sample only where the class is open, such as the other statements of a rule across the tree, or a class over an unbounded domain of keywords, spellings or constructs, and the hand-back says so and says where the part of the class its list did not reach stands. Fourth, the hand-back names each part's method and what it listed, calls a sample a sample, and names what the party did not finish as not done; neither the part a sample did not reach nor what was not finished is the proofer's share. Fifth, the proofer reads that account as a claim and verifies it on its own, and a statement the closed set held that the account missed is a finding on the method, as a sample handed back as a whole list is. The rule's text stands in `skills/agent-contract/SKILL.md` *12 A defect belongs to a class, so enumerate the class*, and this item keeps no copy of it.
2. **`agents/crafter.md`, a new rule under *Rules*, placed before *A fix pass appends close blocks and writes no phase record*, and *Job* gains `changelog.md` in every fix pass.** The rule holds the issue's item 1 for the crafter in a phase and in a fix pass, and item 2 for its hand-back. After its last commit and before it hands back, the crafter reads every hunk of its range's diff with the paragraph or unit around it, a case's name, docstring and comments among them. It reads whole each file of `seal/threads/<work-item-id>/` that states the present: `routing.md`, the three frame files, `gaps.md` and `changelog.md`. It fixes in its range every statement HEAD contradicts in a file its *Job* lets it write, and *Job* names `changelog.md` in the last phase and in every fix pass, so a fix pass fixes the fragment's statements too. It names each one that stands in a file it does not write, `routing.md` or any line of the frame but its Status cell, in the hand-back with the coordinate and the fact HEAD gives, for the orchestrator to fold. That set is closed (rule 12). Then it finds the other statements across the tree of each rule its range reworded or removed, by `git grep` on the rule's words and by its `check:` target, corrects each or says why it stands, calls that list a sample and says where the part it did not reach stands. What it did not finish goes to `gaps.md` `## Not done` with why (G12). The rule carries `check: tests/text/test_a_moved_rule_leaves_its_definition.py`. Its text stands under the bold lead **Before you hand back, your range and the work item's records are read whole against HEAD.**, and this item keeps no copy of it.

The crafter's list is its reading of a file it does not write. After the fold commit, the orchestrator reads that commit and each file of the work item's directory that states the present whole against HEAD, as contract rule 12 asks of the party that fixes. Where that reading finds a statement in a file the crafter writes, `gaps.md` or `changelog.md`, the orchestrator hands it to a fix pass by the crafter that wrote the work item's last build or fix commit, resumed or spawned fresh as `skills/code-review/orchestration.md` *Orchestrator: a phase is a fresh crafter, and a fix pass resumes the implementer* states, before it spawns the next round or phase. The paragraph's other sentences stay.

It cites contract rule 12. "The round that follows reads that list against the diff" becomes a sentence saying the round reads that account as a claim.
