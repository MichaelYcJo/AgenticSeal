### Changed

- **This repository's suite reads the facts a reader acts on in the rules it covers, and no longer pins a rule's wording, across the 56 modules #1237's measure listed (#1233).** The measure listed 506 asserts that compared a literal of four words or more with text standing in `docs/`, `skills/` or `agents/`. Each case holding one was read whole. 107 asserts stay, each with a comment above it naming the fact it pins and who reads that fact: a citation a reader resolves, a command, flag or value a reader copies, a spelling a script or a parser matches, or a program's output. The other 399 pinned a phrase of a rule and leave, and 110 cases leave with them. `tests/text/test_the_handoff_names_the_form_it_ran.py`, `tests/text/test_absence_claims.py` and `tests/text/test_a_probe_does_not_outlive_its_round.py` held only such phrases and are deleted. 61 cases are renamed to say what they still check, and the suite's duration file follows every removal and rename. A rule reworded with its meaning unchanged no longer turns these cases red, and the proofer reads a rule's wording at review, as `docs/what-a-script-gates.md` *The checks* states.
- **Nineteen `check:` lines in the agent definitions and two skills name a reader that still reads their rule (#1233).** The lines are in `agents/crafter.md`, `agents/framer.md`, `agents/proofer.md`, `agents/tracer.md`, `skills/code-review/SKILL.md` and `skills/legacy-parity/SKILL.md`. Each named a module that, after the ruling, held no case reading the line's rule. It now names the module that still does, or reads `check: none reader:` with the orchestrator at each hand-back or at each framer or tracer report. No instruction an agent follows changed. Two lines under `docs/` change at the settle after the release, through this work item's rule changes: the round-count line of `docs/the-review-record.md` *The run* names the settling session at the dry run's judgments, and `docs/the-session-hooks.md` *Registration* names `tests/hooks/test_dispatch.py` alone. `tests/text/test_a_gate_that_fails_says_so.py`, and three asserts kept so that a `docs/` line keeps a reader, stay until a follow-up issue states those lines' changes.
