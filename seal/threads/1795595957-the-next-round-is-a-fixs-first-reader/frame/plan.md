# Implementation Plan: the next round is a fix's first reader (#1615)

## Technical context

The tree as read at `650f9aec4`.

The issue asks for two things, and the tree already holds most of the second. The core's row *No fix that no round reads* (`skills/core/SKILL.md:113`) gives a fix pass that touched the work item's records alone, and closed findings located in them alone, to the sealer; `skills/code-review/scripts/chain_check.py#records` holds that row exactly through `#located_in_records` and `#records_only` (`:976`, `:983`); `skills/code-review/orchestration.md:122` says no round follows such a pass; and `agents/sealer.md:105` to `:110` is the sealer's read of it. What the tree lacks, by `frame/design.md` G3 to G5 and G18: `docs/running/the-run.md:11` and `skills/code-review/orchestration.md:143` still order a round after a regression's records-only fix; the orchestrator has no stated way to read whether a pass is the sealer's, and decides it by eye; and the sealer reads such a pass only against what its finding asked, not for the sentences it made false.

The first ask, record axes, meets a rule that already stands outside the table: `agents/proofer.md:116` to `:136` has every round read the records whole against its target. The table at `skills/code-review/SKILL.md:72` to `:83` is what the proofer walks, "Fix the axes before starting and cover all of them" (`:70`), and it has no record row. `tests/text/test_review_axes.py:6` to `:8` records why no case pins the table's rows.

Writer-side mechanisms from the same release already act on records before any reader: `hooks/edit-leaves-copies.py` (#1612) names, at each `Edit`, the places in the work item's records that still hold the replaced words; `seal pr-body`'s quote check (#1612) refuses a `pr.md` quote its record does not hold; contract rule 19 (#1613) governs what a change writes; and #1614's fold order makes a frame fold after the last round a records-only commit (`frame/design.md` G16). This work adds the reader's half and changes none of them.

The failure scenario of the chosen approach, six months on: a records-only pass after the last round rewrites a fact in `frame/plan.md`, and the same fact stands in other words in `trail/phases/phase-2.md`. The sealer's `git grep` on the fact's words misses it, as any search on words is a sample, and the sealer seals. No round reads the pass, so the stale sentence ships in the records and in the pull request body's copy of them. Before this work a verifying round might have read it, at the price of a round; G17 and G18 measure how rarely such a round found anything. Scope item 7's row reads exactly this case at the next release: a records defect a later reader found in a sentence a records-only pass wrote and a `sealed` verdict passed.
