## Scope

In:

1. **`skills/code-review/SKILL.md` *Comparison axes* gains the record rows below**, four of them, each a row of the existing table whose *Compare* cell names what is read against what and the check: the read or the command the round runs at its target. The rows stand together, and the sentence opening "The last two rows" names *Concurrency & atomicity* and *Security* by their labels instead (G8). One sentence under the table says that the record rows are walked over the records `agents/proofer.md` *Every round reads the work item's records that state the present, whole, against its target* names, which says which records and how a dated section reads (G7), and states none of that rule.
   - *Record against the code*: each sentence of those records that states what the code does or where it stands, against the code at the target. Its check opens each coordinate the sentence names, a path, a `path#name` or a backticked path with an italic heading, and reads whether it stands at the target and says what the sentence says.
   - *Record against record*: each fact a record states, against every other statement of it in the work item's records. Its check is `git grep -n -F '<the fact's words>' -- seal/threads/<item>/` at the target, a sample under contract rules 12 and 19, as *A paste-ready fix reaches every statement of the fact it repairs, or names where it stops* already says of a finding's search.
   - *Record against another work item's frame*: each rule, section or file the records cite that another work item of the release changes, against that item's frame: the unsettled designs' `## Rule changes`, which with the policy root make the policy in force (`skills/implement/SKILL.md` *Document layout — two roots, three lifetimes*), and the rows of this item's `frame/plan.md` *Overlap with the release*. Its check is `git grep -n -F '<the section's heading>' -- 'seal/threads/*/frame/design.md'` at the target, outside the work item's own directory.
   - *Figure against its text*: each image or drawing a record holds, a drawn screen among them, against the sentences that say what it shows, and against the code that renders it where the work builds that code. Its check opens the image.
2. **`agents/sealer.md` step 4, the paragraph opening "A finding of the last record that reads `fixed <commit>`", reads the records-only pass the way a verifying round reads a fix.** Beside reading the record at HEAD against what the finding asked, the sealer reads `git -C <root> diff <the last record's target>..HEAD -- seal/threads/<item>/`: each sentence the diff adds or rewrites, and each other statement in the work item's records of a fact the diff changed, listed with `git -C <root> grep -n -F '<the fact's words>' -- seal/threads/<item>/`, a sample under contract rules 12 and 19, against HEAD. A sentence HEAD contradicts is a `withheld` reason naming its coordinate, as any disagreement is. The paragraph cites `agents/proofer.md` *Every round reads the work item's records that state the present, whole, against its target* for how a dated frame section reads, and restates none of it.
