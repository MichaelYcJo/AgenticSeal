### Changed

- **A round compares the work item's records with the code, with each other, with the other work items' frames and with their own figures (#1615).** `skills/code-review/SKILL.md` *Comparison axes*, the table every proofer walks, held rows about code alone, and a rule outside it had each round read the records whole. In releases 0.0.2 to 0.0.4 of a repository that runs the chain, 8 of the 17 findings a first round missed compared a record with a fact or with another record, with that rule standing. The table now has the rows *Record against the code*, *Record against record*, *Record against another work item's frame* and *Figure against its text*, each naming what is read against what and the read or command the round runs at its target. They are walked over the records `agents/proofer.md` *Every round reads the work item's records that state the present, whole, against its target* names.
- **The sealer reads a fix pass that touched the work item's records alone over its diff (#1615).** Such a pass has the sealer as its only reader, by the row *No fix that no round reads* of `skills/core/SKILL.md` *Judgment is not licence to skip a party*, and the sealer read it only against what its finding asked. In the same releases 43 of the 72 defects a commit after round 1 made stood in records. `agents/sealer.md` step 4 now also reads `git diff <the last record's target>..HEAD` over the work item's records: each sentence the diff adds or rewrites in the records `agents/proofer.md` *Every round reads the work item's records that state the present, whole, against its target* names, and each other statement there of a fact it changed, listed with `git grep`. A sentence HEAD contradicts is a `withheld` reason naming its coordinate. `agents/proofer.md` names the sealer as the first reader of a sentence a fix pass writes after the last round.
- **The orchestrator reads whether a round follows a fix pass off the chain check (#1615).** `skills/code-review/orchestration.md` *Orchestrator: the run ends with a verifying round* has the orchestrator run `chain_check.py --worktree`, the read the proofer runs before it hands back, once the pass's round record is committed. A round follows where the output names that record with the words "a verifying round is owed". Where a line names that record with other words, the party that wrote the line repairs it and the orchestrator reads again. The orchestrator opens `skills/code-review/gate-to-pull-request.md` where no line of the output names that record. A close the orchestrator doubts goes into the sealer's spawn prompt where no round follows the pass. Before, it judged by eye.
- **A regression's fix pass that touched the records alone takes no verifying round, and neither does such a pass for a `must` after the row that judges the run excessive or for a `reopens:` finding (#1615).** The same section owed one in each of the three cases, the regression's whatever the count, though the core's row gives such a pass to the sealer. Counted on 2026-11-25 over this repository and one other that runs the chain, the two such rounds after #1507 found nothing.

`docs/running/the-run.md` *The run* and `docs/running/the-agent-set.md` *What each party writes, and when* state the rule from the settle after this release, which writes them from the work item's `## Rule changes`.
