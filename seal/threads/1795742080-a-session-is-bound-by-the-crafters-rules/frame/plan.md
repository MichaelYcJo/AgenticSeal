# Implementation Plan: a session is bound by the crafter's rules (#1624, #1628, #1629)

## Technical context

The tree as read at `99841677c`.

The work builds on one file both builders already hold and on the files that send a party to a rule.

- `skills/implement/SKILL.md:120-204`, `## Procedure` with *1.* and *2.*, is where the builder's rules land. The crafter opens the file at step 1 (`agents/crafter.md:33`), and the orchestrator reads it beside `skills/implement/orchestration.md` (`skills/implement/SKILL.md:21-24`).
- `agents/crafter.md:86-173` holds the rules that move, and `:17-84` the *Job*, the order and the *Never* that cite them afterwards.
- `skills/implement/fix-pass.md:93-124` cites the rule on the read and holds the account's two sentences.
- `skills/code-review/orchestration.md:23-124` says who builds a phase and who makes a pass, and `skills/implement/orchestration.md:23-36` and `:181-205` hold step 1 and the reading of the frame against the issue.
- `skills/implement/scripts/seal.py:112-173` holds `TOOLS`, and `lib/gitcmd.py:90` and `:238` hold `argv` and `git`, through which a script starts git.

Constraints the build meets:

- A file two parties open names its actor by role (`skills/writing-style/SKILL.md` *에이전트와 검사가 읽는 글은 한 가지로만 읽히게 쓴다*). The moved rules say "the builder", and say "the crafter" or "the orchestrator" only where the two differ.
- The moved rules keep their bold leads, so a citation by lead resolves after the move, and `tests/behaviour/test_a_cited_section_stands_in_its_file.py` holds each citation against the file it names. `skills/implement/SKILL.md` gains no heading and renumbers none.
- A text case pins a fact a reader acts on and not wording (`docs/running/what-a-script-gates.md` *The checks*, the row of the cases that read prose).
- `tests/behaviour/test_docs_line_wrap.py` holds the prose width of the files it lists, `agents/crafter.md` among them.
- A script that reads git builds each command by `lib/gitcmd.py#argv` and takes paths from `-z` output (`docs/running/what-a-script-gates.md` *Which gate a script holds*).
- A new test file declares its `TARGET` and stands where `seal layout` puts it (`CONTRIBUTING.md` *The test layout*).

What breaks in six months. First, a builder runs `seal range` over a large phase, pages through part of the file, and quotes the command in its account. The account is then true of the command and silent on the reading, and no record tells the two apart. The signal is a round finding located in a paragraph or a record the range's file printed; `frame/questions.md` Q1 holds that count. Second, the paragraph bound misprints a prose file that uses no blank lines, such as one long table, and the hunk's block is the whole table. The cost is a longer file, not a missed line. Third, `skills/implement/SKILL.md` grows by the moved rules, and an orchestrator that never builds reads them once per session. The signal is the settle's reading of what the builder paragraph caught against that cost.
