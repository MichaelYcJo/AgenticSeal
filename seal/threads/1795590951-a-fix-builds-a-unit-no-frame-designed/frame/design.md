## Scope

In:

1. **`skills/implement/fix-pass.md` gains a section, `## A fix that needs a unit the frame does not name waits for the fold`, after *A paste-ready fix is read before it is applied*.** It is the one home of what a unit is and of the fix pass's stop.
   - A unit is a new name or path that the frame holds nowhere: a file or a directory; a function, a class, a type or a module-level name, private helpers among them; a hook's entry; a `seal` subcommand or flag; a configuration key; a field of a record's form; or a heading of a skill, a document or a template. A name or path is new where the tree at the round's `target:` holds no part by it.
   - The frame holds a unit where `frame/design.md` *Scope* or *Data & interfaces*, or the `frame/plan.md` row of the phase that built the code, writes the unit's name or path. A helper whose job the frame states, but whose name the frame does not hold, is a unit.
   - Two kinds of change add no unit. One is a change to the body of a part the tree already holds, whether or not the frame names the part, such as a branch added to a function or a sentence added to a section. The other is a part only the tests read, such as a test module, or a helper, a fixture or a constant in one. So a fix that writes a helper's code into an existing body is not stopped: that is the stop's stated limit, and the round after reads such a fix as it reads any fix.
   - Where a finding's fix needs a unit the frame does not hold, a paste-ready fix's among them, the fix pass builds no part of that fix and writes no close block for the finding, which stays open. It fixes the other findings. Its hand-back names the finding, the unit's name or path, and why none of the sections the second bullet lists holds it.
   - Once the orchestrator has committed the framer's fold, the fix pass that follows builds the unit to the fold's design and closes the finding, and its `answer:` names the *Scope* item the fold added.
   - The section ends with `check: tests/text/test_the_rules_have_one_owner.py`.
2. **`skills/implement/orchestration.md` *Orchestrator: the order inside a ticket*.**

The orchestrator sends such a finding to the framer with the round's other decisions before the fix pass where the finding's paste-ready fix or grounds show the unit, and sends it when the fix pass's hand-back names it otherwise.
   - The paragraph opening **The framer writes a decision's fold** is the one home of the narrowing rule. A fold whose source is what a fix or a build changed removes the frame sentence HEAD contradicts, or narrows it to what HEAD still holds, and writes no new account of what was built, since the code, its docstring, the phase record and the finding's close block hold that. That fold is the orchestrator's, of a statement a crafter's hand-back names, and the framer's, of a round finding that says a frame sentence no longer holds.
