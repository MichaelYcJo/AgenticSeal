### Changed

- **A fix pass no longer builds a unit the frame does not name: the finding stays open until the framer's fold has designed the unit (#1614).** A unit the fix pass designed and built in one act reached the next round with no judge but its builder. In each of two repositories that run the chain, counted on 2026-11-25, a fix commit that added a unit was followed by a later finding on what it closed about twice as often as one that added none. `skills/implement/fix-pass.md` *A fix that needs a unit the frame does not name waits for the fold* says what a unit is, private helpers among them, and when the frame names one. Where a finding's fix needs such a unit, a paste-ready fix's among them, the fix pass builds none of that fix, writes no close block for the finding, fixes the rest, and its hand-back names the finding, the unit and why no section of the frame names it. Once the fold is committed, the fix pass that follows builds the unit to the fold's design and its `answer:` names the *Scope* item the fold added.
- **The orchestrator sends such a finding to the framer, before the fix pass where the paste-ready fix or the grounds show the unit, and after the hand-back otherwise (#1614).** `skills/implement/orchestration.md` *Orchestrator: the order inside a ticket* counts the finding among the decisions folded before the next crafter spawns or is resumed. `skills/code-review/orchestration.md` *Orchestrator: a phase is a fresh crafter, and a fix pass resumes the implementer* has a hand-back that leaves a finding open for the fold end no pass, and the one commit of the round record follow the hand-back that leaves none open. Row 2 of the route table in *A regression is routed by comparing the fix with the frame* sends a regression whose fix built a unit the frame does not name to the framer's fold before the fix pass.
- **The framer's fold designs the unit as a first frame designs one (#1614).** `skills/implement/fold-and-reframe.md` has the fold write a *Scope* item naming the finding, the unit's interface, each input it takes and each place it applies, listed from what produces the defect, an acceptance row, and a `## Removes` row or why nothing goes, and weigh the unit against not building it, a stated limit, and a change to a unit the frame already names. The framer may reject it with grounds.
- **A fold whose source is what a fix or a build changed removes or narrows the frame sentence HEAD contradicts, and writes no account of what was built (#1614).** Before, a fold rewrote a frame sentence that says what the work does with the fact HEAD gave, and the restatement was a finding in two rounds of #1612. `skills/implement/orchestration.md`, the paragraph opening **The framer writes a decision's fold**, holds the rule, and `skills/implement/SKILL.md` *Document layout — two roots, three lifetimes* and `skills/implement/fold-and-reframe.md` cite it.

`docs/running/the-agent-set.md` *What each party writes, and when* states the order and the narrowing from the settle after this release, which writes them from the work item's `## Rule changes`.
