### Changed

- **Contract rule 19 has a change write no count and no universal about the tree that a later change makes false (#1613).** A count of files, rows, callers or places, or a claim that every one, none or only one of them holds something, restates a list without the list's words. So the `git grep` rule 19 already asks of the next change does not find it, and the sentence stays false until a round reads it. In releases 0.0.2 to 0.0.4 of a repository that runs the chain, 14 of the 72 defects a commit after round 1 introduced were such a sentence, written by the crafter's fix pass, the framer's fold and the orchestrator's record edits. Rule 19 now asks the text to name the members, or cite the list, the section or the command that lists them, and lets a count stand only in the sentence that opens its list or beside it. A universal that states a rule stays, and so does a count stated of a named commit, release or dated measurement. The rule reaches every text a change writes: records, documents, comments, docstrings and paste-ready fixes. Rule 19 keeps its number.
- **The steps where a party writes after round 1 cite contract rule 19 (#1613).** `skills/implement/fix-pass.md` *A paste-ready fix is read before it is applied* says a paste-ready fix that writes such a count fails the fix pass's reading, so the fix pass writes it in its own words. `skills/implement/fold-and-reframe.md` has the framer's fold write none. Step 1 of `skills/implement/orchestration.md` *Orchestrator: the order inside a ticket* has the orchestrator open rule 19 beside rule 21 for the text its commits write, its record edits, `pr.md`, *Judgments of the run* rows and a merge's resolution among them. The paragraph of `skills/code-review/orchestration.md` on a mechanism whose edges each fix pass chased cites the rule in place of its own clause on counting examples.
- **`agents/framer.md` no longer holds its own count rule (#1613).** The paragraph opening **A count of a list is stated once, beside the list.** bound the framer and the frame files alone, and the framer now receives the rule from the contract it preloads.
- The version moves to 0.39.0.

`docs/running/the-agent-set.md` *One contract every agent receives, and one definition each* states the rule from the settle after this release, which writes it from the work item's `## Rule changes`.
