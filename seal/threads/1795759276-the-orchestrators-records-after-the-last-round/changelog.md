### Fixed

- **The drain commits a row bound for a place outside the work item's directory before the round that reads it (#1630).** `skills/implement/orchestration.md` *Orchestrator: drain the rows before the broad run, and keep the records* had the orchestrator move every unresolved row out once the last round record had no finding open. Its table sends a row waiting on prerequisite work to `seal/follow-up.md`, and a row that needs a decision into a policy document where the work item's subject is the policy root, and the chain check's records arm refuses a commit to either after the last round, so `seal gate --preflight` refused the step the section asked for. The section now has the orchestrator commit such a row before the round that reads it: a leftover a crafter's hand-back names before the round after that phase or fix pass, and a finding deferred in a round record before the verifying round where another finding of that round takes a fix pass. A row whose destination stands inside the work item's directory, or that makes no commit, such as an issue or a comment on one, still moves once the last round record has no finding open and before the preflight. A row that first arises once no round is to follow is weighed: its commit outside the work item's directory costs the verifying round, and a home that needs no such commit costs none. The check keeps its rule, and a case in `tests/skills/test_chain_check.py` pins that a commit touching `seal/follow-up.md` alone after the last record is refused, naming the commit, the path and the verifying round owed. `skills/code-review/orchestration.md` *Orchestrator: the run ends with a verifying round* no longer counts a `seal/follow-up.md` row committed after the last round among the homes that cost no round.
- **A row of *Judgments of the run* gives no inventory of a change (#1639).** In release 0.39.2 the orchestrator's rows that went false mostly restated what a fold commit changed: a number of lines, a list of what the framer folded, a word for the kind of fold, or which commit made a change. `skills/implement/orchestration.md` *Orchestrator: the order inside a ticket*, the paragraph opening **The orchestrator writes its row before it sends a decision**, now says that a row names any commit only as a coordinate and gives no inventory of a change, whether a commit holds it or it is still to come, as a fold's is when its row is written: no number of lines, sentences or items, no list of what the change rewrote or will rewrite, and no word for the kind of change a fold made or will make. What a row sends, the findings and the files or sections a fold or a fix pass is given, is its decision and the decision's coordinates, and not that inventory. `git show <commit>` is that account once the commit exists. A row whose judgment is about a change, such as a regression's route or the title's prefix, states as its grounds what the change means against the document it judges against, and no inventory of it. The row for a fold states the decision as it was sent, and the orchestrator changes it after the fold only where the framer's hand-back, a round's finding or the sealer's verdict names a defect in it, or the framer rejects the decision. The rows stay text: a check of the records arm, a `seal` subcommand, a hook and an agent field were each weighed and rejected, and the work item's design states the decision for the settle to write into `docs/running/the-run.md` *The run*.
