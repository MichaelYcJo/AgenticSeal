## Scope

In. Each item names the owner's principle it follows: one source, a clear criterion, split by target, one job per document.

1. **What each record is for, who writes it, who reads it, which facts it is the home of, and its fate** stand in Table A under *Data & interfaces*. No record is removed whole; two are reshaped, two lose a section, and the rest are kept as they are (one source; split by target).
2. **The templates lose every section or cell that can hold only a copy.** `templates/records/sdd-design.md` loses `## Open questions`, whose one content was a pointer at `frame/questions.md`. `templates/records/sdd-plan.md` loses `## Summary`, which restates the design's *Scope*. `templates/records/sdd-phase.md` becomes its title and `## What this phase found`: the field table copied the file name and the plan's Status cell, and `## What this phase was asked` copied the plan's Delivers cell. `templates/records/sdd-gaps.md` becomes `## Scope confirmation`, `## Not verified` and `## Not done`: `## Why this work exists` restated the changelog fragment, `## Where spec and implementation diverged` copied the phase records, and `## Fed back into the design` has no writer. The five other templates are unchanged (one source).
3. **`gaps.md` gets one writer, one trigger and one test that say the same thing.** The party that builds writes it from its template when the first phase closes, and every later phase and fix pass adds to it; no plan row asks for it; `tests/behaviour/test_chain_hooks_hardening.py#test_every_spec_directory_that_reached_the_ladder_has_an_overview` holds, at every phase boundary, that a directory with a frame has one. `agents/crafter.md`'s Job and step 8 and `skills/implement/SKILL.md` *3.* say that moment; the test's docstring says why it is the first phase (a clear criterion). The `## Not verified` table starts as the no-change block where nothing is unverified yet, which `seal unverified` already accepts.
4. **A departure from the frame is written in the phase record that made it**, in `## What this phase found`, a removal the frame did not name among them. The crafter's rule *Each phase closes with its record*, `skills/code-review/SKILL.md`'s sentence on `gaps.md`'s divergences, `docs/the-reader-registry.md` and `docs/the-agent-set.md` say so (one source). This decides the half of #1045 the issue took in.
5. **What a review round holds a record to.** A wrong line of a work item's record is a `should` finding where a script reads or copies it: a line the check reads, a row `seal unverified` reads, or a line `seal pr-body` copies into the body; a wrong sentence an agent reads and acts on is already `should`. A sentence that restates another record's fact is a finding only where it has drifted, and the answer is removing the copy and citing the home, never correcting the copy. A `fixed <commit>` of the last record whose commit touched only `seal/threads/<id>/` owes no verifying round: `chain_check.py` passes it and the sealer, which reads every record at HEAD, is its reader; a `rejected` finding and a `fixed` that touched any other path still owe the round (the other half of #1045). The three-fix rule moves to `skills/implement/SKILL.md` *2. Implement test-first, and let each rule name its test*, read by the crafter at its step 1 and by a session that builds under `Implementation | the session`; `agents/crafter.md` keeps the application, that a stop is handed back as the architecture question. It is not the core's: the core states values and the signals of non-convergence, and says itself that a rule shaped "when X, do Y" can only be executed (split by target; one job per document).
6. **The marks and labels** are weighed in Table C, each with its reader, its catch record and its cost. Kept: the `check:` line, the three labels, the `Framed` and `Reframed` marks, the round grammar whole, and the `Seal-Routing:` trailer, which is weighed again at the settle after 0.25.x against the hygiene runs' record. Removed: the phase record's `Commit` row, and the plan's `Approved` line, which no code reads and whose whole history is findings about its own shape; reading `frame/plan.md` and spawning the build is the approval, and the phase's Status cell records that it happened. `agents/framer.md` gains the rule the owner directed: a design that adds a mechanism, a gate, a check, a hook, a CI step, a rule, a line grammar, a label or a mark, names in `## Removes` what it takes out or why nothing can go, with that one's catch record, and a step that was blocked or failed is answered by removing or citing its cause, never by a new mark. The framer is the agent that decides a mark, and `CONTRIBUTING.md`, a person's document, stays #1149's.
7. **The measure.** At the settle, before the records leave, four counts per work item are read from its round records with the command under *Data & interfaces*: findings, findings a `regression:` or `reopens:` line names, findings whose every `location:` path is under `seal/threads/`, and rounds. The reading goes to the `flow-baseline` log. Baselines: 0.24.0's four items, 17 recurring of 36, 12 in records alone; 0.25.0's one item, 3 of 10, 1 in a record alone. A release whose records-alone count does not fall says the structure did not remove the copies, and #1020's check is the next step.
8. **`skills/implement/orchestration.md` holds what an orchestrator acts on in every run.** *Orchestrator: which of these acts runs itself* moves whole to `skills/implement/acts.md`, read by a maintainer and by its test. *A work item that commits in more than one repository* moves whole to `skills/implement/multi-repository.md`, opened where a work item commits in more than one repository, its heading keeping the `Orchestrator:` marker so the acts table and its test read it. The reasoning paragraphs inside *What the answer writes* go to the policy root through `## Rule changes`: the `Implementation` criterion's grounds to `docs/the-agent-set.md` *What a spawn costs*, and the rows' shape, the `Answer pressed` row's purpose and the four combinations to `docs/the-review-and-parity-arms.md` *The declaration, and where the check went*, whose own table already holds three of the four and whose *There is no standing waiver* already holds the waiver paragraph. The rows, the criterion in one sentence each and one sentence pointing at each moved body stay. `wc -w` before and after is recorded in the phase record (#1046's shape).
9. **The pull request body opens with the orchestrator's part**, in the repository's `Commit and pull request language`, above the line `skills/code-review/scripts/pr_body.py#HEADING` holds, and the records' copy follows below that line inside one `<details>` block whose `<summary>` names the copy, with a blank line after `<summary>` and nothing inside folded again. `seal pr-body` writes the part below the line and keeps the part above byte for byte; `--check` compares the part below; the sealer reads the part above as the orchestrator's. `agents/sealer.md` step 2, `skills/code-review/orchestration.md` *Orchestrator: the pull request opens once the sealer seals*, `skills/commit-pr-convention/SKILL.md` *Pull request bodies* and `docs/the-review-record.md` *The guarantee* move together. A case pins the order and the fold. The squash commit keeps the block's tags as text; that cost is taken for a body a teammate can read from its first screen.
10. **Every document named by the issue, read against the core, with its reader and its decision**, stands in Table B. The decisions that change a file: `agents/proofer.md` stops naming `CONTRIBUTING.md`; `agents/framer.md`, `agents/crafter.md` and `agents/sealer.md` change as items 3 to 6 and 9 say; `skills/implement/orchestration.md` splits as item 8 says; `README.md`, `README.ko.md` and `seal/README.md` describe the phase record as what one phase found.
11. **The release.** `changelog.md` with a `### Changed` entry and a `### Process changes` row per change in the way of working; `.claude-plugin/plugin.json` to `0.25.1` in the fragment's commit; and the issue's last bullet read as `git diff --shortstat` outside `seal/threads/`, since the work item's own records are additions by construction.
12. **The orchestrator reads the frame against the issue before the first build spawn, and settles what it finds with the framer in one exchange** (the owner's decision of 2026-10-30, in this run). Wherever a framer drew the frame, after it is committed and before the first crafter spawn, the orchestrator reads `frame/design.md` and `frame/plan.md` against the issue's decisions and its *Done when*, looking for where the frame is weaker than the issue asks, departs from its direction or would grow the review load, and sends each reading to the framer as a proposal with grounds and coordinates, never as an instruction. The framer answers each with agree, agree with a change, or reject with grounds, folds what it agreed into its three files, and writes each rejected proposal as one row of `plan.md`'s *Alternatives considered*; the orchestrator edits no frame file. One exchange is the bound: what is left after it stands as the framer's judgment and is named in the orchestrator's part of the pull request body, because each party judges its own target and a second round would be the orchestrator overriding the framer (split by target). No new record and no new mark: it is guidance, `still a sentence` in the acts table, and the counts of each run's proposals, agreements, agreements with a change and rejections go with the framer segment's reading to the flow log, where the settle weighs it against its cost. Its home is the place item 6 empties, `skills/implement/orchestration.md` *The plan is approved at the spawn*, rewritten as *The frame is read against the issue at the spawn*; the framer's half is one rule of `agents/framer.md`; the norm is one rule of `docs/the-agent-set.md` (`## Rule changes`). Its first instance is this run: four proposals, one agreed, two agreed with a change, one rejected (`frame/plan.md` A14b, A18).

Two kinds of record. A round record, a phase record, `trail/gate.md` and `guarantee.md` are history: each names the commit it is about and is never amended; the next record of its kind answers it by reference (`confirms:`, `reopens:`, `regression:`, `; earlier run:`). The frame, `gaps.md` and the plan's Status column are the work's present state and are amended in place: a decision is folded into the sentence it changes, a `## Not verified` row is closed by the word `closed`, a Status cell takes its commit.

The three keeps. Git keeps every commit and, after the settle, each removed record at `<commit>:<path>`. The release notes, the GitHub Release at the tag, keep what a user needs: each fragment and the process rows. The work record keeps the grounds of each decision until the settle. The pull request body's copy of the records, which the squash commit keeps, is the records' one text that survives the settle without a `git show`.
