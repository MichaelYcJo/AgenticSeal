## Data & interfaces

No schema, endpoint or file format changes.

- `seal_stamp.SPRITE` is no longer a module attribute. `seal_stamp.build()` reads the sprite through `read_sprite(SPRITE_PATH)` and raises its `ValueError` where the sprite is malformed.
- `seal_stamp._same_commit(one, other)` and `seal_stamp.ref_is_commit(ref, base)` follow *Scope* item 5. A person sees the change in two spellings of `--base`. A hex spelling of 4 to 6 characters now prints `<ref> @ <commit>` on the `BROAD GATE` lines and the panel's `base` row. An uppercase spelling of 7 or more characters now prints the commit alone.
- `broad_gate.sibling_module` goes. A module that raises while the gate loads it now ends the gate in Python's traceback at exit 1, as any other exception in the gate's code does. The exception is `chain_check.py`, which `#rounds_rows` loads inside its own `except (Exception, SystemExit)`: its raise leaves the rounds count alone and the run goes on, the rule `Test_rounds_rows.test_refuses_to_stop_a_run_where_the_check_will_not_load[raises]` pins.
- `seal stamp`, `seal stamp --from` and `seal stamp --guarantee` beside a malformed sprite: the first two exit 2 with `SPRITE_MALFORMED`'s line where they exited 1 in a traceback, and `--guarantee` queues where it exited 1.
