## Scope

In, each item numbered as the coordinate `frame/plan.md` *Phases* cites:

1. **`skills/implement/fix-pass.md` *The narrow set, then the read of the range* binds the reading to whoever makes the pass, the crafter or the orchestrator, by role (G6).** The routing row does not bound the reading. The paragraph gives it as a case of the orchestrator's, beside a second case, and calls both cases of the rule rather than its limit. First, the orchestrator makes every fix pass where `routing.md` answers `Implementation` with `the session` (G3). Second, the orchestrator writes a fix after the last round in every route, which `skills/code-review/orchestration.md` *Orchestrator: a phase is a fresh crafter, and a fix pass resumes the implementer* gives it and *Scope* item 3 cites (G4, G7). The paragraph keeps the citation of `agents/crafter.md` *Before you hand back, your range and the work item's records are read whole against HEAD* as the rule's one home. It then says what that rule's *Job* and hand-back stand for in the orchestrator's pass:
   - *Job*: in its fix commits, the orchestrator corrects every statement HEAD contradicts in its range, in `gaps.md` and in `changelog.md`. A statement HEAD contradicts in `routing.md` or a frame file, the orchestrator folds as it folds one that a crafter's hand-back names. The fold goes in a commit of its own, after its fix commits and before it appends the close blocks, as `skills/implement/orchestration.md` *Orchestrator: the order inside a ticket* states (G5).
   - Hand-back: the method of the read, each statement across the tree that the read leaves standing with why, and where the part a sample did not reach stands go into the `answer:` of the close block of the finding they concern. Where they concern no finding, they go into the `answer:` of the last close block the pass appends. Where the pass appends no close block, because no finding names its fix, they go into `gaps.md` `## Not done`. The fix of a sealer's verdict that `docs/forms/the-round-record.md` calls "a fix pass no finding named" is such a fix, and it is the orchestrator's in every route. `## Not done` already holds what was within reach and left with its grounds, the verifying round reads `gaps.md` whole, and the sealer reads it at step 4. What the pass did not finish goes to `gaps.md` `## Not done` too, as the crafter's does.
   - The paragraph ends with a `check:` line naming the case of item 5.
2. **The opening paragraph of `skills/implement/fix-pass.md` names the crafter and the orchestrator as the parties that make a pass, and gives the routing row as the case where the orchestrator makes every pass.** It also says that what the file has a crafter's hand-back name for the orchestrator, the orchestrator acts on itself in its own pass, by the step of its own procedure that acts on that hand-back. That reaches the last paragraph of *A fix that needs a unit the frame does not name waits for the fold* (G12): in its own pass, the orchestrator sends such a finding to the framer once the stop meets it, before the pass that builds the unit.

The paragraph opening **Two commits after the build are yours, not the crafter's** gains one sentence that cites `skills/implement/fix-pass.md` *The narrow set, then the read of the range* for the reading a fix the orchestrator writes after the last round makes (G7). The sentence stays a citation and restates neither the reading nor its mapping. It stays in this paragraph because this paragraph gives the orchestrator that fix, and the orchestrator writing it opens this paragraph and not `fix-pass.md`, which only a fix pass opens (G1).
4. **`skills/implement/orchestration.md:89-94` names who makes the fix pass that a statement found in `gaps.md` or `changelog.md` goes to.** It is the party that wrote the work item's last build or fix commit: the crafter, resumed or spawned fresh as item 3's section states, or the orchestrator where `routing.md` answers `Implementation` with `the session`.

The existing case `test_the_fix_pass_follows_its_own_file_and_the_form_states_the_close_block` keeps holding the citation of the crafter's rule.
