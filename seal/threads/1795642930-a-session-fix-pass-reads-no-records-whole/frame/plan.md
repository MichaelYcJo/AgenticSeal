# Implementation Plan: a session's fix pass reads its range and the work item's records whole

## Technical context

The tree as read at `d399c049f`.

The work builds on one rule and the three files that send a party to it. The rule is `agents/crafter.md:150-173`, *Before you hand back, your range and the work item's records are read whole against HEAD*, which stays as it is. `skills/implement/fix-pass.md:85-91` sends the crafter to it after the narrow set. `skills/code-review/orchestration.md:23-102` says who makes a fix pass and what the orchestrator does with the pass's hand-back. `skills/implement/orchestration.md:21-212` says how the orchestrator folds a frame or `routing.md` statement that a hand-back names. `frame/design.md` G1 to G5 give what each says.

Constraints the build meets:

- `fix-pass.md` is opened by two parties, so its new text names the crafter and the orchestrator by role, with the routing row as a case and not as the bound, never "the session" or "you" (`frame/design.md` G6).
- The new text adds no heading, so no test that reads a section by its heading moves. `tests/text/test_the_rules_have_one_owner.py:426` reads *Orchestrator: a phase is a fresh crafter, and a fix pass resumes the implementer* by that heading, and `tests/text/test_a_moved_rule_leaves_its_definition.py:177-226` reads the crafter's rule by its bold lead and `fix-pass.md`'s citation of it. Both stay.
- A text case pins a fact a reader acts on, a path, a code span or a cited section, and not wording, as the design of `seal/threads/1794383427-asserts-pin-literal-phrases/` states in its `## Rule changes` for `docs/running/what-a-script-gates.md`.
- Each changed file keeps the prose width that `tests/behaviour/test_docs_line_wrap.py` holds where it lists the file.
- The release 0.39.0 rules a session's pass meets are already bound to whoever makes it: contract rule 19 (`frame/design.md` G11), the stop for a unit the frame does not hold (G12), and the sealer's reading of a records-only pass (G13). Only the stop's hand-back sentence is crafter-shaped, and *Scope* item 2 maps it.

What breaks in six months. An orchestrator under `Implementation` `the session` reads *Scope* item 1's paragraph, makes the read, and writes a short `answer:` that names no method, so the next round cannot tell a read made from one skipped. The signal is a `regression:` or `reopens:` finding located in the records of a session-built item after 0.39.1, read as `frame/design.md` G8 read the earlier ones. `frame/questions.md` Q1 holds that measurement. A second failure: the `answer:` of the last close block grows into a list that the sealer reads as the account of one finding. The signal is a sealer's `withheld` reason naming a close block whose account concerns other findings.
