### Changed

- **The proofer's eval cases now rebuild trees the current plugin reads as its records root (#1229).** The eight replay and seeded cases under `evals/behaviour/proofer/` were cut from round records in the layout before 0.23.0, `seal/specs/<item>/rounds/`. Each rebuilt a tree whose `seal/README.md` does not open with the marker the gates look for, so the current plugin did not read that tree's `seal/` as its records root, and the suite measured a tree no user of the current release runs. They are replaced by the eight cases that `evals/scripts/replay_targets.py --count 4` and `evals/scripts/seed_defects.py --count 4` write from round records under `seal/threads/<id>/trail/rounds/`. The replay cases are `replay-1793419351-r4`, `replay-1793457435-r3`, `replay-1793411151-r2` and `replay-1793419350-r2`. The seeded cases are `seeded-55b66958`, `seeded-ea73e94f`, `seeded-5fd6b88c` and `seeded-7a8899d5`. Each regenerates byte for byte with `--case` from the release tags. The suite's replay and seeded cases now hold 14 `truth-` graders where they held 27, one for each replay case.

- **A seeded case no longer credits a missed seed to a report that names only the real finding (#1229).** A seeded case plants one defect in a tree a shipped work item's round 1 reviewed, and grades the proofer's report with one `truth-` grader for the seed and one for each real defect round 1 fixed. Each real defect's grader accepts lines near the first coordinate its finding's `location:` names, and `evals/scripts/seed_defects.py` kept the seed away from that coordinate alone. A finding often names several coordinates, so the seed could land near a later one. In `seeded-55b66958`, the seed sat within 15 lines of the finding's fourth coordinate, and the `location:` line round 1 wrote for the real finding passed the seed's grader too. A run that missed the seed and reported the real defect was scored as finding both. The generator now keeps the seed more than 15 lines from every coordinate a real finding names: a line, the whole of a range, a unit's lines, or the whole file for a bare path. Regenerated this way, `seeded-55b66958` takes a seed at another line. `seeded-bf9efcba`, whose seed sat inside the range its finding names, has no line left to seed and leaves the suite, and `seeded-7a8899d5` takes its place. The 10 entries of `evals/scripts/recurred_truths.json` that widened or added truths of two of the old cases leave with them, because the generators stop on an entry whose case is gone. The old cases and those entries stay readable at `v0.27.1`. A proofer comparison now runs both sides from the commit that lands this change, because `noninferiority.py compare` refuses a pair whose suites differ. No recorded side is lost by this, since no side taken before 0.27.0 compares with a later one. Whether the proofer reads a new case's records and writes its record where the prompt says has not been measured: no paid eval run was taken in this release.
