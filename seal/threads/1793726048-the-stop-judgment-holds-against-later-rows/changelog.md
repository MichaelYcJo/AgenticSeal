### Changed

- **A row that judges a review run excessive now binds every row after it (#1288).** In #1216's run, the row after round 6 made round 7 the last and sent its `should` to `seal/follow-up.md`. The row after round 7 overrode it on a rule no file of the tree states, and the row after round 9 overrode the next stop on the drain's first row. Rounds 5 to 10 opened six findings, none of them a `must`, for about 670k tokens of proofer spawns. `skills/code-review/orchestration.md` *Orchestrator: the run ends with a verifying round* now has the orchestrator weigh what the next round can catch against what it costs, at any round. Three finding rounds are where that weighing is owed, and they are no cap. Once a row judges the run excessive, a `must` keeps its fix pass and its verifying round, and every other finding takes a home that costs no round. A later row spends no round on a finding that is not a `must`, whatever document it cites. A regression that is not a `must` takes a home too: from the stop row on, only a `must` takes a route of *A regression is routed by comparing the fix with the frame*, so no other regression goes to a fix pass or to the framer, whose redesign would end the stop. A fix that changes no assertion, such as a test's comment or name, rides in a fix pass another fix already owes. After a stop row, the first row of `skills/implement/orchestration.md` *Orchestrator: drain the rows before the broad run, and keep the records* does only a `must`, or an item whose fix is confined to the work item's records, now. The settle narrows `docs/running/the-run.md` *The run* the same way: its first rung of homes, its sentence on routing a regression, and its sentence that called the three finding rounds the cap that stops a loop. Every fix outside the work item's records is still read by a round.
- **A gap in a case that holds no fact the frame states is no `should` by default (#1288).** Rounds 5 to 9 of #1216 each opened one `should` in a test of a helper that moved with its body. `skills/code-review/SKILL.md` *The record* now weighs such a case by what the release ships without the fix. Such a case is one whose assertions hold no behaviour that a *Scope* item, an acceptance row or a `check:` line of a rule the diff changes states, and a fact that only a *Grounding* row states does not count. A parameter that pins nothing, a guard of code the diff did not change, or a comment or name that claims more than the case pins opens no finding, and the round names it in one sentence of its prose. The gap stays a `should` where its grounds name a behaviour the diff changed that no case then holds. `agents/proofer.md` *Each new test is read against its group* cites the bullet for such a case.
