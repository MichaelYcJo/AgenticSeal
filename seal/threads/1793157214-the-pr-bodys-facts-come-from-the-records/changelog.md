### Changed

- **A pull request body routed through the review chain opens with the work item's records, copied by `seal pr-body` (#1044).** The new command `seal pr-body <item> --body <file>` writes the part of the body above the line `## Not from the records`. That part holds the changelog fragment, the plan's phases table and the paths of the phase records, each review round's `target:` line with the `severity:`, `verdict:` and `answer:` lines of its findings, the broad run's record, the *Not verified* and *Not done* sections of `gaps.md`, and every table line of `frame/questions.md`. The command copies lines and writes no count, summary or rewording of its own, and a record that is missing is named by a fixed sentence. Run again on the same file, it rewrites the part above the line and keeps the part under it byte for byte.
- **The orchestrator writes the body after the broad run's record, and writes only under `## Not from the records` (#1044).** It runs `seal pr-body` once `trail/gate.md` is committed, then writes under the line what no record holds: the motivation, the `Closes` line, the broad run's printed figures and the attribution line. Where its part needs a record's fact, it names the record's path instead of restating it. It runs the command again before every later spawn of the sealer and before every edit of an open pull request's body.
- **The sealer checks the copied part and reads only the orchestrator's part (#1044).** Its step 2 first runs `seal pr-body <item> --body <file> --check`. On any exit but 0 the verdict is `withheld`, and its reason is the first line the command printed. On exit 0 the sealer reads the part under the line as the orchestrator's account, and the part above it as no party's.
- **Where `Commit and pull request language` and `Record language` differ, a body is in two languages (#1044).** The copied part is in the language of the records, and the orchestrator's part in the language the row names. No repository's config changes.

### Removed

- **The orchestrator no longer writes the body by hand before the broad run (#1044).** Most of a hand-written body restated a record, and in 0.22.x 23 of the sealer's 27 withheld runs named the body. The sealer no longer weighs the whole body as one party's claims, because only the part under the line is written by hand.
