### Removed

- **The table of the orchestrator's acts, and the test that held it, are gone (#506).** `skills/implement/acts.md` listed every heading marked `Orchestrator:` in the orchestration files against what notices when the act is skipped, and `tests/behaviour/test_every_orchestrator_act_names_its_delivery.py` held the list against those headings. Every message the test could report was about the table itself. A search of the issues since the table shipped found several that name it, such as #1109, #1163 and #506. Each names a defect in one of its rows, what it cost its readers, or a limit of the table, and none came from an act the table listed as one nothing notices when it is skipped. Meanwhile it cost a row for each new marked heading, a review finding on a stale row, and re-reading in later phases. The decision it was kept for, which act to give a mechanism next, starts from a miss measured in a run's records, and the table supplied none. So the rule in `docs/running/the-agent-set.md` *What a spawn costs* that stated it goes too, with the check-line target in `docs/layout/the-pact.md`, the two orchestration files' citations of it, the test case and the parser paragraph that read it, and its entries in the line-wrap list and `.test_durations`. Nothing replaces it. The three gaps #506 named, a docstring's list of cases, the marker's two meanings and an act outside the listed files, end with it: the `Orchestrator:` marker now means only that a section reaches the orchestrator and no agent. The old table stays readable at `git show v0.31.0:skills/implement/acts.md`.
