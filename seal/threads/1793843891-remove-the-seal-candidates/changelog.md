### Removed

- **`assets/seals/candidates/` is gone: its eleven files drew marks that no release carried (#1375).** They were the bee and two section signs, each as a chart or sprite, a terminal stamp and a preview, and the bee's vector seal. The owner chose the horned owl over the bee in #857, and the little owl has been the mark since 0.25.1. No code in the tree read the files. The `## Candidates` section of `assets/seals/README.md` goes with them, with its three entries and four image links. The README's preview rule now names the three previews its Pillow 12.3.0 measurement covered that remain: the gold lily's, the red lily's and the placeholder S's. `TRADEMARKS.md` no longer names the candidates or a `.txt` file under `assets/seals/`: its first list item names every `.svg`, `.png` and `.ans` file there as the emblem each release carried. An installed plugin drops the eleven files at its update to this release. Git's history keeps them, for example `git show v0.32.2:assets/seals/candidates/bee-28-pixel.png`.
