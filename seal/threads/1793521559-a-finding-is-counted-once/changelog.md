### Fixed

- **The settle's four numbers count a finding once, by its own block (#1223).** Step 2b of `docs/release-checklist.md` counts, for each released work item routed through the review chain, its findings, the findings with a `regression:` or `reopens:` line, the findings located in records alone, and its rounds. The step defined them as a `grep -h` reading and named no command, so each settling session spelt its own pattern. Since round records hold `seal` blocks, a close block may carry a `location:` line naming where the fix went, and that grep counted such a finding's `location:` twice. A new script in this repository, `python3 .github/scripts/finding_counts.py`, prints the four numbers as the table the `flow-baseline` issue's comments carry. It reads each round record through the pull-request check's own reader, `skills/code-review/scripts/chain_check.py#record_findings`, and counts a finding in records alone by its finding block's `location:` alone. A work item one of whose records that reader refuses a line of reads `not read` in its three finding cells, and the refusal is printed under the table. `chain_check.Finding` gains the field `located`, the finding block's own paths; no exit code or message of the check changes. The script ships to no user. The step's own text changes at the settle after this release.
