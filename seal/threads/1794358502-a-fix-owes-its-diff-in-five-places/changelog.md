### Changed

- **Contract rule 12 no longer defines what a fixing party reads whole, and no longer addresses the proofer, so every agent preloads 822 bytes less (#1472).** Every agent spawn preloads `skills/agent-contract/SKILL.md`. Rule 12 held seven sentences that only the crafter and the proofer act on: which files a fix reads whole, why that list is never a sample, what is not the proofer's share, and how the proofer reads the fixing party's account. The rule now keeps the class, the three methods that make a list whole, and where a list stays a sample. It still says what a hand-back names: the method and what it listed, a sample called a sample, where the part it did not reach stands, and what was not finished. The contract holds 12,364 bytes, where it held 13,186.
- **The crafter's rule *Before you hand back, your range and the work item's records are read whole against HEAD* gives its own reason the set is closed (#1472).** It named the files and pointed at rule 12 for the reason. It now reads "That set is closed, and reading it whole is rule 12's second method, so none of it is handed on as a sample." Its last sentence is new: "Neither that nor the part a sample did not reach is the proofer's share." `agents/crafter.md` grows by 112 bytes.
- **The proofer's rule *Every round reads the work item's records that state the present, whole, against its target* holds the proofer's sentence on a fix's account (#1472).** The rule now ends with "The implementer's account of what its own reading of its diff and of these records listed is a claim you verify on your own: a statement that reading held and the account missed, or a sample handed back as a whole list, is a finding on the method, even where every instance on the list was fixed." The rule listed the records a second time after step 3 and now cites step 3. `agents/proofer.md` grows by 218 bytes.
- **Rule 19 and two skill files cite rule 12 for what it still states (#1472).** Rule 19's restatement of the sample becomes "rule 12 calls that a sample." `skills/implement/orchestration.md` and `skills/implement/fold-and-reframe.md` each cited rule 12 "as contract rule 12 asks of the party that fixes" for a reading of whole files, and now name "the second method of contract rule 12".
