# implement, the framer's fold and reframe

The framer opens this file before it answers a decision to fold into its
committed frame, whether it was spawned or resumed for the fold, and before
it answers a `regression:` the orchestrator routes to it. No other party
acts from it, so the rule below addresses the framer as you.

**You have two routes back: a decision's fold, and the review chain's
regression.** A decision made after the frame is committed comes back to
you as a fold: the orchestrator resumes you, or spawns you again, with
every decision then pending, each with its grounds and the coordinates it
stands on (`skills/implement/orchestration.md` *Orchestrator: the order
inside a ticket*). Answer each decision with agree, agree with a change, or
reject with grounds, as `agents/framer.md` *An orchestrator's reading of
the frame is answered in one exchange* has you answer a proposal, and write
each rejected decision as one row of `plan.md`'s *Alternatives considered*.
A decision the owner made, the version move and a prevention home are
folded as they were made, and where you judge that one of them conflicts
with the tree, your hand-back names the conflict.
Before you write what a decision says, open each coordinate it cites, such
as an issue it restates, as contract rule 5 asks of a fact that reaches you
in prose. Then rewrite in place every sentence of your three files that the
decision changes, reading each file whole, as contract rule 12 asks of the
party that fixes. Read the orchestrator's *Judgments of the run* row for
each decision with `plan.md`, where it stands written and not yet
committed, and name any defect in it in your hand-back. You write no line
under your mark, no Status cell and no *Judgments of the run* row, and a
section dated to the commit you read keeps its date. Spawned fresh for a
fold, you read the three frame files, the coordinates the decisions cite,
and the diff since the frame's commit over the paths the decisions name,
and you do not repeat the wide reading of `agents/framer.md` *Order of
work* step 4. Your hand-back names each sentence you changed, by
coordinate, and each statement the fold contradicts in a file you do not
write. On the review chain's route, the orchestrator spawns you again when
it routes a `regression:` to you, with that run's round records. The row
*The framer* of the framer's route in `skills/code-review/orchestration.md`
*A regression is routed by comparing the fix with the frame* states what
you then rewrite, and the `Reframed` line you add under your mark is the
run document's: `docs/running/the-run.md` *The run* owns that rule.
